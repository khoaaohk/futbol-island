# Futbol Island: reasons to return

Research and recommendations only, September 13, 2026. These ideas were not implemented. The separately requested store expansion is implemented locally.

## What already exists

The shipped lesson catalogs contain 96 lessons, 918 teaching steps and 193 quiz questions across futsal, 7v7, 9v9 and 11v11. The island already offers exploration, talking NPCs, vehicle tricks, ball impacts/juggling, three arcade minigames and a phone-controller match. Eight introductory quests record visits, completed lessons, quiz answers and equipment. The store now has 30 equipment options locally, with 15 new additions and two actions per item.

Progress and equipment are saved in the current browser. Quiz progress records unique correct answers, not a history of mistakes, confidence or spaced review. Equipment is currently free to try. Coaches' IDP and Coaches Board remain Coming soon. The volleyball rally is currently an NPC activity, not a player-controlled minigame. The production transfer feed worked when checked; the scores feed returned unavailable.

My assessment: the strongest opportunity is to connect these activities into short goals, personal progress and rewards that players can display. More scenery alone gives a weaker reason to return once someone has explored it. This is a product hypothesis, not a measured retention result for this game.

## Recommended additions

| Priority | Idea | Concrete experience | Reason to return | Relative scope |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Today's island circuit | Pick three short tasks: find the spare player in one play, perform a flight trick through a marker, and beat an NPC's juggling challenge. Aim for a satisfying 3–5 minute visit. | A fresh, immediately understandable goal using existing activities. | Medium: scheduling, task evidence and a small progress card. |
| 2 | Costume collections | Choose an outfit to work toward; finish a few learning, exploration or skill goals to earn it permanently. Keep existing free equipment available. | Visible self-expression and a clear next reward. | Medium: outfit geometry, previews and wardrobe evidence. |
| 3 | NPC rivals and friendships | A skater challenges your trick score, a coach remembers the topic you practiced, and a pier regular has a new short task. Give each character a recognizable personality. | Familiar characters develop with the player instead of repeating their first greeting. | Medium: persistent dialogue stages and activity triggers. |
| 4 | Personal bests and challenge links | Race your own prior flight route, improve a target-kicking score, or send a friend the same arcade challenge and compare results. | A concrete performance to beat and something easy to share. | Medium; personal records first, shared verification later. |
| 5 | A personal coach in IDP | Select a role or goal: “Find space as a midfielder.” Offer a short session with a new idea and a review of an earlier mistake. Gradually reduce hints. | The next visit feels relevant and demonstrates improvement. | Medium–large: concept tags, attempt history and review scheduling. |
| 6 | Weekly Island Cup | A week-long trio of activities: one arcade score, one skill course and one football decision. Award a permanent pennant or outfit accessory for participation milestones. | Shared themes and collection progress; everyone can participate on their own schedule. | Medium, reusing existing games and task tracking. |
| 7 | Football this week | Imani introduces a verified result; a short, curated tactical situation connects it to a lesson. Rafi offers a sourced transfer story plus a position-learning prompt. | Real football gives returning visits fresh context. | Medium plus recurring editorial work; fix score-feed reliability first. |
| 8 | A personal locker | Display favorite kits, earned pennants, completed position cards and an arcade trophy shelf. Let players choose what visitors see in a shareable image. | Earned rewards become a place and identity the player cares about. | Medium; begin as a locker screen rather than a new 3D building. |

For the weekly goal, let players complete any three sessions over the week. Keep earned progress when they miss a day and let themed rewards return later. Give clear stopping points: “Circuit complete—your next challenge is ready tomorrow.”

## Costume agent recommendations

Use a separate outfit selection that preserves the player's chosen character, skin tone, body and hair. The current Captain and Explorer choices occupy the character slot, which makes them a poor model for a flexible wardrobe.

Suggested collection:

- **Island Captain:** gold trim, captain's armband and distinctive boots; a passing-learning reward.
- **Beach Explorer:** bucket hat, tropical shirt and shorts; landmark and NPC tasks.
- **Match Official:** striped shirt, wristbands and a whistle; rules challenges.
- **Street Freestyler:** cap, colorful jacket and knee pads; juggling and trick goals.
- **Space Striker:** compact helmet, padded suit and colored boots; mixed weekly activities.
- **Island Shark:** shark hood and small fin over a football kit; a collection milestone.

Start with the first four. Existing procedural body parts and small attached accessories are a reasonable fit; huge mascot heads, long flowing capes and simulated cloth need more work. Every outfit must still fit seated rides and the helicopter rotor. The new Iron Man flight equipment is separate from this proposed general-purpose wardrobe.

## Research behind the recommendations

- Nintendo describes earning Nook Miles from varied island activities, redeeming them for items, and displaying rewards through customization. This is a useful precedent for connecting Futbol Island's exploration, NPCs and wardrobe. [Nintendo: Animal Crossing activities and rewards](https://play.nintendo.com/news-tips/tips-tricks/animal-crossing-new-horizons-discover-tips/).
- Duolingo describes using spaced repetition and accuracy to choose personalized practice, with harder material reviewed sooner. Applying that principle to football decisions would give the IDP a meaningful purpose beyond completion counts. [Duolingo: spaced repetition](https://blog.duolingo.com/spaced-repetition-for-learning/).
- Duolingo's Friend Streak connects people who learn separately rather than requiring simultaneous sessions. An asynchronous challenge or shared weekly goal is a relevant starting point for this island. Its published engagement findings are specific to Duolingo and should not be assumed to transfer here. [Duolingo: building Friend Streak](https://blog.duolingo.com/product-lessons-friend-streak/).

## What I would try first

Start with Today's Island Circuit, a small costume collection, and personal-best challenges. They reuse the island's strongest existing parts and connect “something fun to do” with “something I want to earn.” Add the personal coach next, once field-space explanations are consistently clear.

The separate field-highlight audit found only 32/918 teaching steps and 52/193 quiz feedback scenes explicitly shade space; 133 authored spotlight cues have no renderer handler. Quiz zones also currently lose their authored dimensions. Those findings remain recommendations after the request to stop building; no highlight changes were retained.

Evaluate these ideas with actual player behavior: first meaningful activity completed, return after one and seven days, voluntary second challenge attempts, and correct decisions on a different version of a learned concept. Do not treat longer sessions alone as success. Cross-device collections and verified friend scores would also need a save/account strategy beyond the current browser-only progress.
