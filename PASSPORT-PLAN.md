# Football Passport pilot

Purpose: reward understanding of football, club culture and player decisions.

## First playable version
- Passport beside Quests, also accessible from Settings and related Store cards.
- Three complete journeys: Barcelona, Arsenal and Bayern. Each reuses its existing mascot story/quiz, adds one current star and one club legend, then an animated pitch decision.
- A correct player question collects that player's card. Story + both player cards + pitch decision earn a club stamp automatically. Existing story completion counts.
- Related items in every existing store category display earned learning badges; they stay free to equip. Mascots connect directly to their journeys. No paid unlocks, timers or lost streaks.
- Progress persists locally and synchronizes across tabs. Wrong answers explain the mistake and allow retry. Replays never duplicate rewards.
- Current player affiliations checked against official club sources on 13 September 2026. Training scenarios are original illustrations, not reconstructions of actual matches.

## Trial checks
Complete a journey without leaving the app; verify each reward and store link; reload and resume; check old story progress; check incorrect answers, keyboard controls and narrow mobile layout. Keep the window size stable between views.

## After trying it
Use feedback before expanding all 23 clubs. Later candidates: NPC chapter entry points, linked full pitch lessons, alternate scenarios for spaced practice, optional cosmetic colors/trails, and cloud sync. Those are separate from this pilot.
