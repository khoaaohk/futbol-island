# Bug squad and firefly stories: facts and sources

Every catch-card story in `lib/town/jobs/bugSpecies.ts` is a real football fact, checked by web search on 20 Oct 2026. Each fact is
listed here with the source the story cites (the story's `source` field), plus any extra source used to check it. The bugs' club
roles, homes and bands are original game fiction, not fact. `tests/bug-species.cjs` checks that every story's source appears here.

## Unsure or left out

- Records that could have changed at the 2026 men's World Cup are written as "at that time" or without "record" (Fontaine's 13 in
  one tournament, Klose's 16, Marta's 17 in 2019).
- Left out because sources disagree or could not be confirmed: the first referee's whistle (1878), the origin of "clean sheet", the
  captain's armband's history, crowd figures for the 1878 floodlit match, and the exact panel/weight of the 1930 final balls.
- The 1909 goalkeeper-colour rule: sources disagree on which body brought it in first; the story only says "in 1909 a new rule".
