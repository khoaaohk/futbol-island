# Endgame: graduations, the Matchday Ferry final, the History Museum and the Coaches Board

**Date:** 30 Sep 2026 · **Lane 2** of the "do all" pass on the game audit (gap G-01 and related) · local only, not committed or deployed.

The island had no ending: finishing a path gave a card pick and a ride, finishing all four said "the Matchday Ferry opens in a future update", and the Ferry, Museum and Coaches Board were "Coming soon". This pass gives each format a graduation, turns the Ferry into a one-off Matchday final, opens the Museum and makes the Coaches Board real.

## 1. Graduation per format

**Rule.** A format graduates when its 12 starter lessons are complete. The rule is the same `lessonEvidence` "complete" rule that Paths, card tiers and ride unlocks use.
- It is saved once, with the date, in `fi2-graduations-v1` (`lib/endgame/graduationModel.ts`, `graduationStore.ts`).
- It is never removed. Lost or changed quiz keys cannot take a certificate away.

**Trigger.** `components/GraduationHost.tsx` (mounted in Town) watches the quest and quiz stores the Paths screen reads.
- It saves new graduations through `syncGraduations`.
- It opens the ceremony at the next calm moment: no lesson, card offer, chat, map, onboarding or other modal.
- Each graduation has a `seen` flag. It is set when the ceremony closes, so the ceremony shows **exactly once per format**.
- **Retroactive:** a save that finished paths before this update graduates on its first load and gets its ceremony once.
- When several paths graduate at once, one ceremony shows a tab per certificate, never a queue of popups.

**The ceremony** (`GraduationCeremony.tsx`) uses the riso venue art, finite confetti, and a certificate card listing the 12 lessons learned. It shows:
- **Rewards:** that path's graduate cap colours, the certificate, and a Hall of Fame entry. It also mentions the ride and Icon card pick that path completion already gives.
- **Next up:** the next format to try, following 7v7 → 9v9 → 11v11, with futsal as the court detour. **Go** switches Paths to that format.
- When all four have graduated, the next-up slot shows **Board the ferry** instead.

**Certificates** (`lib/endgame/certificate.ts`, `components/Certificate.tsx`):
- On screen they are DOM and SVG.
- **Save picture**, **Share with a grown-up** (the system share sheet, when it accepts files) and **Print** draw one canvas on demand, 1600×1130 landscape. The canvas is freed straight after.
- **No personal data:** no name, age, photo or location. A certificate shows only the format, the ideas learned and the date.

**Where certificates live:**
- the backpack's **Trophy shelf** (a registered backpack category, `lib/endgame/backpackTrophies.ts`);
- the Museum's **Hall of Fame** case;
- the **Coaches Board** badges.

## 2. The Matchday Ferry final

**Unlock condition:** all four starter paths graduated (48 lessons). The ball hunt is **not** required: the audit's "4/4 paths" milestone, kept achievable.

**Locked Ferry** (`FerryPreview.tsx`):
- The badge reads "Opening soon · finish your paths!" (Lane 1's `OPENING_SOON_LABEL`).
- Each path shows its graduation or progress (x/12).
- The copy says how to unlock, and an **Open Paths** button goes to the first unfinished format.

**Open Ferry:**
- The spinning lock over the in-world ferry is hidden. Town toggles its visibility on the graduation-change event, and `world.ts updateFerry` no longer re-shows the lock sparkles.
- The Paths harbour card (`QuestLearningPath.tsx`) switches from "Opening soon — finish your paths!" to **Board the ferry**.

**The final** (`MatchdayFinale.tsx`, rules in `lib/endgame/finale.ts`). It teaches by review:

1. **Boarding.** A ferry crossing, and the plan for the day.
2. **Coach's exam.** Two picture questions from each format's own starter lessons, 8 in total.
   - Each question comes from a different lesson.
   - The kinds are tap-spot, best-pass, pick-picture, drag-to-zone, what-next and true/false.
   - They render with the lessons' own `VisualQuestion`.
   - A wrong answer shows that choice's explanation, then **Try again**, so the trophy is always reachable.
   - Each round loads only its own lesson file (the cached catalog) and warms the next one.
3. **Draw the pass.** Two problems (open lane, switch play).
   - Drag from the ball, or tap a team-mate.
   - A defender within 4.2 units of the line blocks the pass.
   - The right answer is the open lane furthest forward ("forward if you can").
4. **Trophy ceremony.** Only first-try answers are counted, and the result is framed positively ("…and you fixed every other one yourself").
   - Rewards: the **Matchday Champion** cap colours and the **Island Diploma**.
5. **Credits.** A thank-you, "Starring: you", and the 48 lessons learned, grouped by format. They scroll by hand, with no auto-roll.

The first finish keeps its date. Later rides count toward `runs` and keep the best score. **Ride again** is a review mode.

A live-match finale was considered and rejected for now. It would wake the island's 3D match sim behind the finale, which costs heat. The exam reuses the lessons' own mechanics instead.

## 3. The History Museum

The Museum is `Museum.tsx`, with its data in `lib/endgame/museum.ts`. It is a walk-in hall of 12 exhibit cases in four galleries. Each gallery opens with a collection the island already has:

| Gallery | Opened by | Cases (need) |
|---|---|---|
| The Laws of the Game | hidden balls found | 1863 first Laws (free), 1891 penalty kick (10), 1970 yellow and red cards (25), 1992 back-pass rule (50), 2018 VAR (80) |
| World Cup history | player cards | 1930 first World Cup (10), 1991 first Women's World Cup (30), futsal and the 1989 Futsal World Cup (60) |
| Balls and kits | pop-up books (the starter book counts) | laced leather to size 5 (2), the 1970 Telstar (3), shirt numbers and Law 4 colours (4) |
| Hall of Fame | graduations | your certificates (1) |

**How a case works:**
- Tapping an open case zooms into it, like the vending machine's close-up view. It shows the facts, a "Take it to your game" line and its sources.
- A locked case says exactly what to collect and how: "Collect 7 more hidden balls to open this case (3/10). Find hidden balls around the island…".
- The first case is always open, so the building is never a dead end.

**Sources.** Every case (except the player's own Hall of Fame) cites its sources: Wikipedia articles, the IFAB Laws of the Game and the FIFA Futsal Laws.
- Live re-fetching was blocked in this session. So, like the Konbini magazines, each fact is kept to a well-documented core: dates, winners, Law numbers.
- These facts match what the Konbini (1930 World Cup, ball size) and the vending machines (Telstar, laced leather) already teach.
- **Please re-check before extending a case.**

**Placement.** The museum is an in-world dialog over the sleeping island, not a document boundary like the Konbini. Reloading the island after a short museum visit would cost more (asset parse, shader warm-up) than the hall itself, which is static DOM.

## 4. Coaches Centre

- Lane 4 owns the IDP card and the grown-ups view.
- This lane made the second card, the **Coaches Board** (`CoachesBoard.tsx`), live. It shows:
  - graduation badges (tap one for its certificate);
  - the coach's next goal on every path (the next lesson, with **Go**, or "Graduated, try Go deeper");
  - the Ferry status;
  - the team shapes each path pins when it graduates: futsal 1–2–1, 7v7 2–3–1, 9v9 3–2–3, 11v11 4–3–3.
- The in-world sign under COACHES now reads **NOW OPEN** (it read OPENING SOON).

## 5. Rewards and economy

- **Coins:** none added. Path completion keeps its existing 75 learning coins and Icon card pick. The final pays no coins, so there is no late-game inflation (ECONOMY_PROPOSAL §7 already notes the missing late sink).
- **Cosmetics:** five Headwear colour options (`CAP_REWARDS`): Futsal, 7v7, 9v9 and 11v11 Graduate, plus Matchday Champion.
  - They are coloured like each format's home pitch and vending machine.
  - They are gated in `isCustomizationUnlocked` and `sanitizeCustomization`, so a locked colour can't be worn from an edited save.
  - The customizer's lock note says how to earn each one.
  - They are free once earned, with no vending purchase.
- **Collectibles:** certificates and the Island Diploma (Trophy shelf).
- The economy doc (`docs/economy/ECONOMY_UPDATE_2026-09-29.md`) has an addendum.

## 8. Tests

`tests/endgame.cjs` (in `npm test`) runs against the real stores over an in-memory localStorage. It checks:
- **Triggers:** graduation fires once per format; nothing fires at 11/12; a re-sync doesn't fire again; `seen` persists across reloads.
- **Persistence and migration:** certificates persist; they survive lost quiz keys; old saves graduate retroactively with their ceremony once; the dev unlock opens the ferry; corrupt or future-version saves are safe.
- **Ferry:** it opens only at 4/4; the finale can't be recorded while the ferry is locked; the best score is kept.
- **Cap colours:** locked until that path graduates, and dropped from a save until then.
- **Coach's exam:** 2 questions per format from starter lessons, deterministic per seed, only kinds `VisualQuestion` renders. Each draw-the-pass problem has exactly one open-lane answer.
- **Museum:** the first case is free; every case is sourced; each opens by its own collection with how-to-unlock copy.
- **Certificates:** 12 lessons each; the diploma only after the final; no personal data. The Trophy shelf lists them.

Browser runs (Chromium, muted) are at 390×844 and 1280×800 on `?unlock=all&paths=1`, plus a fresh save for the locked states. The script and screenshots are in the session scratchpad (`game-fixes/lane2/`).
