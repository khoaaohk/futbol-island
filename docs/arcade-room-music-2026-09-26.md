# Original arcade room music — September 26, 2026

Local, not deployed. `arcadeRoomMusic.ts` authors a distinct playful 112 BPM four-bar electronic groove: short chip melody, offbeat answering notes, warm bass, soft kick/backbeat and hats. No island music import, downloaded samples or copyrighted recording. The score is synthesized once into an approximately 8.57-second, 24kHz mono AudioBuffer (~0.82 MB), then looped by one BufferSource through one dedicated gain in the room's existing AudioContext. A short boundary taper avoids a loop click. No live sequencer, timer, RAF or additional context.

Music follows `fi2-music-enabled` (true by default) and `fi2-music-volume` (0.04 by default), independently of sound-effect mute/volume. `setMusicEnabled` and `setMusicVolume` persist those preferences. Storage changes and returning from hidden refresh them without polling. A gesture is required before context creation; both disabled buses allocate nothing. Music can run when SFX are muted. Hidden rooms suspend the shared context; preference changes while hidden stop disabled music but never start a new source. Dispose stops/disconnects the loop and gain, releases its buffer, removes listeners and closes the context. There is no asynchronous synthesis callback that could recreate audio after disposal.

`node tests/arcade-room-sound.cjs` passes original-loop waveform bounds and seam, allocation bound, gesture gating, one-context reuse, independent music/SFX controls, muted+disabled suspension, hidden preference changes, and idempotent disposal. TypeScript passed. Browser gesture/audio lifecycle integration and subjective listening are separate checks for the parent integration pass; this test does not claim measured phone heat or speaker balance.
