# Visual direction and storyboard contract

September 19, 2026. Applied from installed `art-director` (getburo/buro-free) and `storyboard` (toyme/storyboard-skill) guidance. User instructions and existing Grit / Regulating Emotions films remain the reference. Skills guide production; they do not establish visual approval.

## One visual world

Colorful, textured paper-cut football explainers. Use recognizable objects to make mental concepts visible: a ball, a boot, woven fabric, a radio dial. No full-body people. Large silhouettes and deliberate negative space; fine detail supports the main action. Match the existing films' cream, navy, blue, pink, orange and yellow vocabulary. Assign colors consistently to the same objects and roles across the whole film.

Each film has one main physical metaphor. Preserve object identity, scale relationships and geography as the camera travels. A new object enters for an explanatory reason, with a visible connection to the previous action. Continuous motion alone is insufficient if the metaphor is hard to understand.

## Plan before expanding

For each short narration passage record:

| Field | Required decision |
| --- | --- |
| Spoken cue | Exact words and their audio time |
| Meaning | What the viewer should understand |
| Visible cause and result | What changes, why, and what shows its effect |
| Action sequence | Usually 3–6 meaningful stages; adjust to the sentence |
| Framing | Starting view, focal point, camera destination |
| Handoff | Which object/action/position connects to the next passage |
| Phone composition | What remains visible above the caption tray |

Movement must reveal information. Build anticipation, perform the action, let its result read, then follow that result onward. Avoid perpetual small wobbling, arbitrary zooms, unrelated symbols and generic transition portals. Do not force a new shot at a chapter boundary.

## Woven Court benchmark

Follow the orange thread into a crossing; show another strand passing over and under it. Move close enough to see the connection. Pull back as a ball loads the fabric and multiple strands share the deformation. Withdraw surrounding support while retaining the same strand and ball; visibly stretch and sag the isolated strand. Reconnect the neighboring strands so the load spreads and the ball steadies. The outcome must read with narration muted.

## Review gate

Review the complete moving sequence with audio, then without audio, on desktop and phone. Check that each narrated idea has a visible cause and result, the eye can follow every handoff, and subjects stay readable under texture. Compare actual frames against Grit / Regulating Emotions. Fix the weakest sequence before copying its approach to other films. Boundary tests and frame counts verify implementation only; they cannot approve illustration quality.

## Night scene application

Warm community football after dusk: dark blue sky, readable warm ground and foliage, warm-white playable pitch, amber path lights. Visible fixtures explain the illumination. Keep the ball, players and markings distinct. Preserve the mobile lighting/shadow budget and inactive-render sleep; measure added work rather than claiming phone cooling.

## Latest Woven correction: recognizable phrase illustrations

Abstract describes the flat, textured treatment, not unreadable subject matter. Use immediately recognizable question marks, speech bubbles, hands, ears, people, footballs and goals. Every narrated paragraph needs several distinct explanatory actions, aligned to spoken phrases. Draw with JavaScript/Canvas vectors so shapes remain crisp through scale changes. Preserve connected object movement and overlapping actions; avoid enlarging tiny raster panels, one picture per paragraph, meaningless ribbons or full-screen color pauses. The final paragraph specifically uses asking → helpful conversation → offer → listen → support → mutual reliance.

## Woven audit refinements

Selected zoom-through connections are requested, but their destination must reveal a distinctly different design. Repeating a net/ball or two-lobe arrangement after a zoom is insufficient. Preserve the shared material/object across the seam, then expose another scale or construction: load-bearing net → broad interlocking ribbon close-up; exchanging roles → angular defensive-space composition.

Foreground colors must remain distinct from the background behind their entire movement. Avoid pink-on-pink and equivalent collisions by retaining navy negative space under the action. Woven thread paths and their overpasses must follow identical geometry without disconnected stubs. Speech bubbles use filled paper silhouettes, not outlined boxes. Hands should be abstract cupped forms with print texture and a restrained crease; literal segmented glove fingers, colored palm badges and bright cuffs were judged too cartoony. Keep the offering/supporting action legible.
