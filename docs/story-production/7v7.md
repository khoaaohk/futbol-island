# 7v7 story production

Local implementation, September 19, 2026. Three original metaphor stories; simple language for developing players, without claiming that one age determines a football format. The surrounding shared player owns grain, captions, audio playback, lifecycle, controls and shape transitions.

| Story | Learning purpose | Words | Scheduled duration | Measured voice duration |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A Place in the Picture | Belonging does not depend on copying teammates or completing every pass | 132 | 60s | 45.19s |
| The Pocket Radio | Notice harsh self-talk, breathe, choose a kind and useful next action | 140 | 60s | 48.62s |
| The Signal Across the Water | Ask a clear question, then practice with support | 141 | 60s | 46.43s |

Each story has six ten-second beats. Voice durations were measured from the finished M4A files with `afinfo`; the remaining beat time is a deliberate visual/breathing pause. The longest clip is 9.084s, so no clip overruns its beat. Complete narration and short labels live in `lib/paths/films/seven.ts`. Per-clip duration manifests sit alongside the audio at `public/stories/narration/7v7/<story-id>/timing.json`.

Temporary narration uses the locally available macOS Samantha voice, requested at 128 words/minute, generated with `say` and encoded offline to AAC 64 kbps mono with ffmpeg. The requested rate is a synthesizer setting, not a claim about measured speaking speed. Voice assets are replaceable and incur no paid generation service. Sandbox speech initially returned empty AIFF files; the authorized native speech invocation produced real audio. All 18 finished clips have nonzero measured duration.

## Choreography

- **Picture:** overlapping irregular mosaic pieces separate, opening a turquoise river around the unchanged blue piece. The picture develops into a pitch; the blue piece moves wide and a ball travels to a teammate. Shapes, river and passing line share one continuous composition.
- **Radio:** a coral pocket radio sits within a wavering jagged signal. Tuning rotates its cream dial and smooths the signal; the speaker enlarges into a pitch-centre composition. A short passing route makes the kind inner instruction concrete.
- **Water:** five broad currents separate two illustrated shores. A small figure raises a scarf above reeds, signal arcs reach a lit window, and a curved cream connection carries a ball across the water. The composition opens into a defending picture with the learner between an opponent and their goal.

Draw functions use a responsive centred 800-unit coordinate system, deterministic global time and bounded loops. They allocate no DOM, timers, animation loops, paths, images, gradients or audio sources per frame; canvas path calls are bounded. The shared renderer must draw only while active/visible and cap DPR/frame rate according to its contract. Reduced motion uses one static composition per narration beat, suppressing continuous wave travel, shake and zoom. Existing shared grain supplies texture without new raster assets.

## Validation and limits

- Isolated TypeScript compilation passes for `seven.ts` and its imported contract.
- Canvas contract sampling at 320, 390 and 1440px, every quarter-second of all stories, in normal and reduced-motion modes: finite coordinates and balanced save/restore; all 18 audio paths exist.
- Narration length measured using native `afinfo`, with individual measurements preserved in each timing manifest.
- Shared browser visual/control/lifecycle checks and the combined production build belong to the coordinator. This document does not claim those checks have completed.
- No deployment performed by this story pass. No real-device iPhone temperature or Safari playback measurements.
