3. In futsal, trust grows through clear actions. After passing, move into view. Give your teammate an angle and a call.
4. Shared responsibility means noticing what the moment needs. One player moves forward. Another offers support behind. Your jobs can change with the play.
5. When possession is lost, blame adds distance. A helpful response brings you together: recover, protect space near goal, and communicate the next job.
6. Ask yourself: what can I do to help someone else play? Offer, listen, support. Trust grows when you can rely on each other.
