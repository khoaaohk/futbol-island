# 11v11 concept-film revision

This revision replaces fictional plots and named characters with direct, second-person explanations of mental concepts. Tone follows the direct question, concrete metaphor, felt difficulty and practical cue structure of the installed Grit and Regulating Emotions films. Six narration beats per film, approximately 130–135 words, intended for natural delivery around one minute. Read narration directly from eleven.ts for neural voice production.

**Audio handoff:** existing Samantha clips and beat timings describe the rejected first draft. They must be replaced/retimed against the revised narration before release. No audio regenerated in this revision. Existing draw functions are deliberately untouched pending new cinematic artwork; their former diagrams are not the visual brief below.

## Shared image-board direction

Generate six cinematic keyframes per film as one coherent visual world. Full bleed, intensely saturated cobalt, burnt orange, warm pink, deep navy and cream; tactile paper fibres, rough pigment, dense screenprint grain. Confident large abstract forms, dramatic cropping, depth from overlapping painted layers, rich negative space. Avoid glossy 3D, stock icons, generic football pitch diagrams, card layouts, scenic children's-book plots and illustrated named characters. No text, lettering, numbers, borders or captions inside the art. Keep the centre legible across landscape and portrait crops. Camera descriptions below are transition instructions: the final animation should travel inside the existing form and discover the next, with continuity of material and colour rather than a dissolve between unrelated posters.

## The Quiet Lantern — quiet leadership

1. **Quiet leadership.** Full-bleed navy void with immense overlapping cobalt arcs curling in from all edges; a tiny irregular cream opening and warm orange inner rim near the centre. Tangible ink grain, dramatic difference in scale. The eye should discover the small opening despite the surrounding visual noise. Camera approaches that opening.
2. **A little light.** Inside the previous opening: a large off-centre molten orange oval with a cream slit sending one broad angular wedge through cobalt layers. Abstract light made of painted paper, no literal lantern or scenic room. Camera moves along the wedge, whose edge curves into a path through the next form.
3. **Notice the need.** A pink folded ribbon has a small interruption between two large folds; the entering cream light reveals the gap while surrounding navy remains dense. Composition makes an overlooked space feel visible. Camera follows the ribbon's edge toward the gap; the light becomes a connecting strip.
4. **Make it useful.** That single cream strip crosses the gap between two great orange and cobalt planes, a precise simple connection inside a busy field of curved material. One clear route, generous scale, no arrows or tactical markings. Camera travels across the strip; it bends into two facing curves.
5. **Listen as well.** Two massive painted crescents, warm pink and orange, face one another around an open cream centre. Small light bands extend from each, with equal visual weight and room between them. Camera drifts through their shared centre as their edges curl outward.
6. **Share the light.** Pull back from inside the curves into a wide field of interlocking cobalt, pink and orange folds. Several cream openings carry the same warm light seen in frame one; each small opening illuminates a neighbour. No hierarchy, no dots-and-lines network. Finish with a slow settling expansion and one strong cream opening holding the centre.

## The Boat and the Weather — control and response

1. **Weather and response.** Huge jagged orange and pink weather-like sweeps cut diagonally across a cobalt field; a small soft cream curve remains visible between them. Wind expressed as thick printed material, not a landscape or literal storm. Camera enters the space between sweeps.
2. **Find your sail.** Inside a cobalt sweep, discover a monumental curved cream sail-like plane with an orange seam. The surrounding navy currents flow at a different angle; the plane can turn within them. No boat, ocean horizon or character. Camera moves toward the seam, revealing that it is flexible.
3. **Make some room.** Extreme close-up of the orange seam separating two crowded navy folds; its centre widens into a quiet pink oval. Pressure remains at the edges while space opens locally. Tactile grain, no blur effects. Camera slows and travels into this breathing space.
4. **Choose an action.** The pink oval stretches into one smooth cream ribbon which turns deliberately through rough cobalt and orange layers. A clear small bend changes its direction; surrounding forces remain visible. Camera tracks the bend, keeping the point of choice central.
5. **Work together.** That ribbon meets a second orange band, and the two broad forms overlap to hold open a passage through navy. Distinct materials retain their identities; neither dominates. Camera moves through the supported passage as its edges become sail-like curves again.
6. **Your next response.** Wider view of the cream curved plane, now tilted with a warm pink support fold beside it. Large cobalt/orange currents continue around it; the image stays dynamic rather than becoming perfectly calm. Hold the plane's clear orientation as the final message: a chosen response within ongoing difficulty.

## More Than a Shirt — identity beyond position

1. **More than a role.** A monumental cream outline loosely evokes a shirt through two angled shoulders, but remains abstract, without number, seams or a literal hanging garment. Within and beyond it, cobalt, orange and pink fields continue across the full image. Camera pushes toward one edge of the outline.
2. **An open outline.** Macro view: the cream edge opens into a thick curved doorway rather than a closed border; vibrant pink and orange forms pass through it uninterrupted. Show paint texture at an intimate scale. Camera travels through the opening as its former boundary becomes a ribbon.
3. **When things change.** Cream ribbon bends under a heavy navy fold, compressing the central orange shape without erasing it. Warm pink remains visible around the pressure. Communicate the feeling of a changed role through tension and proportion, not a sad cartoon. Camera follows the orange shape through the narrowed space.
4. **Ask and learn.** The orange form opens into a fan of broad curved layers with several visible directions; a cream strip joins one pink layer without closing the others. Visual curiosity and expansion, no question marks or map arrows. Camera travels along the chosen strip and discovers its colours in a larger form.
5. **Carry your strengths.** Multiple generous orange, cream and pink shapes extend beyond a central cobalt fold, different scales and orientations, bound by shared pigment and paper texture. Identity as a larger composition, no collage of hobby icons. Camera pulls back through overlapping layers, revealing more beyond the frame.
6. **Keep some room.** Wide full-bleed field of free interlocking forms; the opening shirt-like outline is now a small incomplete cream curve within a much larger colourful world. Orange and pink arcs reach beyond every edge, with substantial cobalt breathing space. End on an expansive composition that implies more remains to discover.
