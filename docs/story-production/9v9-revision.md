## Shared cinematic treatment

These are keyframes from continuous, full-screen abstract films, not six static slides. Use saturated cobalt `#2459E8`, orange `#ED824C`, pink `#E8A1BC`, navy `#172B51` and cream `#F7EACA`, with visible fine paper grain and broad, imperfect printed edges. Shapes extend beyond the viewport. Avoid framing a small illustration on an empty background. No literal pitch diagrams, cartoon characters, boats, houses or illustrated maps. The metaphor lives in scale, rhythm, tension, folds and connected forms.

Each beat has a dominant sculptural shape and room for a short highlighted phrase. Source artwork should contain no baked lettering: live typography remains sharp and adaptable. Keep the focal action around 45% of the frame height; compose for portrait and landscape from the same oversized layers. Preserve breathing room around the top heading and lower captions. Use slow pushes, close crops and shape-to-shape morphs as described below. Reduced motion can hold each resolved keyframe with a brief dissolve.

## Different Tides — six storyboard frames

1. **YOUR OWN PACE.** Full-bleed navy, almost entirely covered by a huge cobalt swell entering from the left and a slower pink swell entering from below. A thin cream gap separates them; neither is presented as a winner. Camera pushes toward that gap as the opening question lands.
2. **DIFFERENT TIDES.** The cream gap expands into the frame. Orange, cobalt and pink bands curve through it at unequal widths and rhythms, like different shorelines seen extremely close. Morph the first swell into these bands, maintaining one continuous edge through the transition.
3. **LOOK CLOSER.** Push deeply into a thick pink band. Previously subtle cream grain lines become bold growing contours, revealing change that was too small to notice from afar. A cobalt crescent opens within the pink rather than cutting to a new picture.
4. **ONE SMALL TARGET.** The crescent curls into a large open ring, wrapping almost the entire screen. A small cream disc pauses at the opening; an orange arc turns gently to reveal a navy pocket beyond it. This visual turn supports looking before receiving, without depicting players or a field.
5. **NOTICE YOUR CHANGE.** Follow the disc through the opening until it becomes a cream field. Two irregular cobalt contours appear, one lightly ghosted and one slightly more open: earlier and current attempts. The newer edge eases outward by a small, legible amount; no ranking, ladder or progress score.
6. **KEEP YOUR RHYTHM.** Pull back just enough to reveal the contours as three connected, screen-filling currents. Orange, pink and cobalt keep distinct rhythms while sharing one cream channel. Slow the motion into an open navy space for the final phrase; end with space remaining ahead, not a finish line.

## The Harbour at Night — six storyboard frames

1. **ROOM TO PAUSE.** Large orange diagonal planes press through a cobalt frame, their cream edges taut like stretched cloth. One navy interval opens between them. The opening question accompanies a slow camera drift toward this space, with no literal harbour illustration.
2. **REST IS NOT WEAKNESS.** Zoom into the navy interval until it fills the screen. A sweeping pink arc settles around a cream oval, suggesting a sheltered space through curved geometry. The orange edge from frame one remains at the boundary, linking motion and rest within one world.
3. **CHECK IN.** The cream oval expands into a softly textured field. Three uneven cobalt strokes, initially crowded and jagged, separate enough to become individually visible. Their movement slows; noticing is shown as making room to see, not as magically removing tiredness.
4. **SAY WHAT YOU NEED.** One cobalt stroke unfolds into a broad ribbon extending toward an orange shape on the opposite edge. A small cream opening travels along the ribbon; the orange form opens in response. Shapes communicate through space and orientation, without characters, speech bubbles or UI icons.
5. **LET THE SAIL SETTLE.** Track along the ribbon into a close view of pink and orange folded planes. Taut diagonals loosen into broad cream curves over navy. The composition becomes spacious and still, with grain and gentle shade giving the folds depth. This is the longest held image.
6. **EFFORT AND REST.** A cream fold lifts into an open curve, revealing cobalt on one side and warm orange on the other. The camera widens to show both active and quiet spaces in one continuous form. Finish on the balanced, open composition; avoid implying that rest must immediately produce a performance reward.

## The Unfinished Map — six storyboard frames

1. **WHAT DID YOU HEAR?** A bold orange stroke meets an enormous navy plane on a pink field. The camera closes in on the contact point; the stroke bends under pressure but retains its color and substance. Suggest the feeling of criticism without crosses, grades or an illustrated mistake.
2. **INFORMATION TO USE.** The navy plane rotates into a cobalt folded edge, revealing a cream opening beside it. The orange stroke becomes a soft pencilled curve tracing that opening. Morph the obstacle into useful information; there is no literal map, compass or route diagram.
3. **ASK FOR A LANDMARK.** Zoom through the cream opening. Broad cobalt and pink forms initially overlap, then separate to reveal one small orange notch. The camera settles on that specific detail as the questions become concrete. One detail becomes clear; the whole screen need not be solved.
4. **TRY ONE CHANGE.** The orange notch stretches into a curved opening through a screen-filling cobalt ribbon. A cream shape turns slightly and passes through the new space. Emphasize the small angle change rather than speed, victory or a ball traveling over a pitch.
5. **YOUR WORTH STAYS.** Push into the cream shape until it fills the frame. A smaller orange line can be redrawn along its surface while the underlying cream mass stays whole. Navy and pink frame its edges. The visual distinguishes an adjustable action from the enduring person without a literal human silhouette.
6. **KEEP LEARNING.** The redrawn line unfolds into several generous orange, pink and cobalt curves, with one selected cream opening continuing beyond the frame. Drift forward along the open curve. End with the form unfinished and spacious, supporting curiosity rather than a stamped answer or completed checklist.
