## Pictogram spec used in every HIGH item below (§1c.4 as clarified)

Not full-scale people: an **abstract cut-paper pictogram** — one torn-edge head disc, one hand-cut torso wedge, two leg strips, optional arm strip; no face, no hands, no feet; printed in the person's role ink at .9 with a navy contour (`contour` 8–10 u, `handCut` 12/40); 300–420 u tall at camera zoom 1 (about a third of the phone safe height), 1–3 on screen, always the largest thing in frame when the sentence is about them (§1c.5). Emotion is the whole silhouette: shoulders (torso wedge) dropped, head disc tilted, arms up, leaning. Use the same primitive in all four stories; the ink, the head shape (round / squarer / taller) and the torn amplitude differ per story so the figures are not identical across the path. The football: a paper disc with the story's own panel treatment at a size a child can name (r ≥ 55 at zoom 1), and the panel treatment must still read as a *football*, not as an eye or a buoy (§1c.3).

## 5. Path-level notes

- **People in every story.** All four stories used a different abstract stand-in for the same three roles (you / teammate / coach). With §1c.4 the path should share one pictogram primitive (spec above) and vary only ink, head shape and torn amplitude; that also gives the 7v7 path a recognisable "cast" a child follows from story to story, which none of the other three paths have yet.
- **Balls.** place-picture (eyeball) and empathy (white dot) fail §1c.3 outright; pocket-radio's pulse is not a ball at all (add one for the "pass" cues); signal-water's buoy needs two faint pentagons. The cross-story anti-repetition rule still holds: five pentagons + pink crescent / band + two pentagons / crescent + net / pulse-seam are four different footballs.
- **Motion budget.** No seam regresses, no reverse zoom, 3+ camera moves per chapter everywhere. Holds > 1.5 s: place-picture ch1, ch5, ch6; pocket-radio ch5; empathy ch1, ch2, ch4, ch5 — eight in the path, all removed by giving the figure something to do on the cue.
- **Bug list.** signal-water ch6 curl dot (135 u white disc crossing the lake). empathy touch not visible. pocket-radio ch1 cap under the title at 844×390; signal-water ch3 coach flag under the title at 844×390.
- **Do not change.** The print language (plates, rings-as-grille, water bands, washes-as-halos), the cue timing, the passages, the touch reactions for place-picture / pocket-radio / signal-water. The user approved the style and speed; every item above changes *what* is drawn, not how it is printed.
