# 9v9 path review — Different Tides · The Harbour at Night · The Unfinished Map · Grit

September 20, 2026. Read-only critic pass on the four 9v9 riso stories as they run in the app (dev build on :8092). Evidence: burst strips at 1/12 s steps for every cue and both seam passages of every chapter, at 390×850 (DPR 2, touch) and 1440×850; 320×568 and 844×390 phone sheets; touch-reaction bursts; per-frame motion metrics (`motion-390.json`: fraction of pixels changed per frame, paper coverage, long holds). Strips live in `scratchpad/riso/critic/9v9/<id>/strip-<vp>-ch<N>.png` and `phones-touch.png`.

Read `reviews/cross-story.md` first. It already rules on everything set-wide that touches this path and those rulings are **not repeated** here, only referenced: the paper-and-navy-pentagon ball in all four 9v9 stories (fix 1), harbour-night's sail / lantern / kite / speech bubble vs boat-weather and quiet-lantern (fix 2: **harbour-night changes**), unfinished-map's equidistant contour rings (fix 3), headline de-duplication (unfinished-map ch3 → LANDMARK, ch4 → NEW ANGLE; grit ch8 → EVERYBODY WANTS or none), headline-over-action in different-tides ch3/ch4 and harbour-night ch2 (fix 7), the navy-night thinning (harbour-night ch3 deck, grit ch9 ending lighter; fix 8), and the touch de-duplication (different-tides and unfinished-map drop their rings, different-tides drops the navy tick).

This review adds what only a frame-by-frame look at the path shows: **the kid test per chapter (bible §1c, new user rule)**, whether the motion performs the words (§4b), the seam passages, and two engine defects.

**Verdict in one line:** Grit is the only 9v9 story a nine-year-old can follow with the sound off (7 of 9 chapters pass); Different Tides and The Harbour at Night fail the kid test in 5 of 6 chapters each because every human idea in the narration (a teammate improving, feeling stuck, being tired, telling your coach, learning from teammates) is carried by a tethered ball, a gauge post, a lantern or a hanging ball; The Unfinished Map is half-way (the map itself reads, the "note frame", the red stamp and the disc/wedge players do not). The fix is the same in all three: put one to three cut-paper player silhouettes on screen where the narration says player, teammate, coach or "you", and make the football things football things.

---

## 0. Kid test method

Sound off, phone in hand, 9 years old. For each chapter I wrote the one sentence the child would say, then compared it with the storyboard's meaning. **FAIL** = the shapes need decoding (a symbolic stand-in, an unnameable object, a person implied by an abstract shape, a football that does not read as a football). **PASS** = the sentence matches the meaning without the caption. **PART** = the picture is nameable but the meaning is not in it.

Path score: Grit 7 PASS / 2 FAIL; Unfinished Map 2 PASS · 1 PART / 3 FAIL; Different Tides 0 PASS · 1 PART / 5 FAIL; Harbour at Night 1 PASS · 1 PART / 4 FAIL.
