## 0. Path-wide defects found in motion (fix once, all six benefit)

**P1 · HIGH · Headline crossfade garbles words (engine, `StoryFilmPlayer.module.css`).** When one headline leaves and the next arrives, both are centred on the same line and overlap for ~0.35 s, printing a third word: "LOUSEFULND" (quiet-lantern, `E/320x568/sheet.png` ch4-mid), "TONEXTER" / "[TOGETHER]" (boat-weather ch6-mid, ch5-mid), "FRUFEARION" (regulate ch3-mid; also `regulate/strips/1440x850-grid/seam2-3-in.png` frames 1–3), "PLAYFEARUGH" (`regulate/strips/390x850/touch-ch3.png` frame 1). My captures are seeks, but the same overlap happens in normal playback at every chapter seam where the previous chapter had a headline (the leave animation is real-time CSS and the arriving pop starts immediately). Fix: the leaving headline must be gone (or clipped to 0 height) before the new one pops — sequence leave (0.2 s) → pop, or slide the leaving word up out of the band while the new one pops in below the band's centre. Also clear the leaving headline instantly on seek.

**P2 · HIGH · Touch reaction invisible in 4 of 6 stories (§4d, §9 gate "touch burst").** The gate only checks `data-draws` increments. Looking at the frames: quiet-lantern (`quiet-lantern/strips/390x850/touch-ch3.png`), boat-weather, more-shirt and reset show **no visible change** at +40/+180/+380/+620 ms at phone size (the yellow cone/flare lands on yellow lantern glass; orange gust streaks land on the orange sail/front; the pulled thread is a 6-unit navy loop lost in the knit; the shaken leaf is yellow on the yellow leaf close-up). Regulate (paper blob + confetti burst) and loss (small blue ring) are visible. Rule of thumb for the fix: the touch mark must be a knockout (paper) or the story's contrasting ink, ≥ 120 units, with a 0.3 s overshoot; test it over the chapter's dominant ink, not over paper.

**P3 · HIGH · Pitch drawn as a blue field with white lines (regulate ch1, quiet-lantern ch3/ch6, boat-weather ch4).** The 11v11 ink set has no green, so the three "football" chapters print a *blue* pitch. A 9-year-old reads regulate ch1 as "a swimming pool with lines" (`regulate/strips/1440x850-grid/ch1-cue2-4.80s.png`). Blue × yellow overprints green in this engine (§2 of ENGINE.md, ≈ #18721a): print the pitch as yellow field + blue tone .6 so it is green, with paper lines. Quiet-lantern's night pitch can stay navy (night) but needs a green band at the touchline so it reads as grass.

**P4 · MED · Lozenge "players" (regulate ch1 orange/blue lozenges, boat-weather ch4 orange/white/purple lozenges).** These are the rejected Astra "dots on a pitch" in riso clothing (`boat-weather/strips/1440x850-grid/ch4-cue2-32.81s.png`, `regulate/strips/1440x850-grid/seam1-2-out.png`). §1c.4 (as clarified): replace with **abstract cut-paper pictograms** — a chunky two-piece silhouette (torso wedge + head disc, one leg wedge for a run), one ink, no limbs detail, poster-scale (≥ 260 units tall), 1–3 per frame. Specific poses are given per story below.

**P5 · MED · Every chapter opens on a 0–2 s near-hold** (scan: quiet-lantern 0.2–2.2 s, reset 3.2–4.8 s, loss ch3 20.8–22.4 s). Chapter openings should carry the residual force of the passage (a settle) — a decaying sway, not a still.

**P6 · LOW · 320×568 title bar covers the top of the art** in every story (the lead object is cut at the top: quiet-lantern ch1 lantern, boat-weather ch2 sail head, regulate ch2 star tip). The safe region is honoured for the tray but not for the title/Done strip; move the composition centre down ~60 units at short viewports or shrink the arrival scale to .84 there.
