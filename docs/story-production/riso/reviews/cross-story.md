# Cross-story art direction review — all 18 riso stories side by side

September 20, 2026. Read-only review against `RISO_BIBLE.md` §1b (unique backgrounds, distinct imagery), §2 (palette), §4c (headlines), §4d (touch), and the three user reference frames (`reference/texture-01..03.png`). Evidence: the per-chapter app captures at 390×850 and 1440×850 for every story (viewed as one row per story, all chapters at phone width, plus the desktop rows), the contact sheets in `scratchpad/riso/review/<id>.png`, every storyboard header, and the `spec` / `headline` / `touch` blocks in `lib/paths/riso/stories/*.ts`.

Verdict in one line: **14 of 18 stories own a genuinely different world; the set feels repetitive on a phone for four reasons that are cheap to fix — the same paper-and-navy football in 8 stories, concentric rings in 6, a navy-night beat in 7, and two sailing-boat / two lantern stories — plus BREATHE used as a headline six times.**

### 1a. Duplicates and near-duplicates across the whole set

**Identical ink triples (§2 says two stories in a path may not share one).**
- 11v11: **reset = quiet-lantern** (yellow / blue / purple) and **loss = regulate** (yellow / orange / blue). Six stories from four inks makes this arithmetically unavoidable (only four triples exist). Accept it, but enforce distinct *dominance*: quiet-lantern is navy/indigo-dominant with yellow light, so **reset must keep blue as an accent only** (it does — the blue neighbour branch); regulate carries all three inks at full weight, so **loss must keep orange as an accent** (the whistle, numerals, wake ring — it does). No change required, but builders must not drift.
- Cross-path: chalk-line = place-picture (yellow / pink / green). Different paths, different dominance (green court vs yellow/pink plates). Fine.

**Same background construction (§1b: only one story may use each).**

Kite-turned and reset are the *same* construction in two colours ("wind bands" — the bible itself suggested both, but the rule still stands). Reset is the weaker (bands are static horizontals; the branch sits on them). See fix 9.

## 2. Does it feel repetitive? (played several in a row on a phone)

Overall: **no for the worlds, yes for the props.** A player who runs a whole path sees six different constructions (plaid → sky → paint → chalk; plates → rings → water → washes; sand → night → paper → strata; wedges → fronts → knit → references → bands → pond). What they *also* see is the same white football with navy pentagons turn up in nearly every story, and the same "dark navy field with a yellow light" and "concentric rings" motifs returning across paths. These are the pairs that feel alike, with the change to make (weaker story changes):

1. **harbour-night ↔ boat-weather** (9v9 then 11v11): the same white-sailed boat on blue water with an orange/yellow sky; in the captures harbour-night ch1 and boat-weather ch2 are interchangeable. Change **harbour-night**: ch1 arrives with the sail already dropping (a luffing bundle on the boom, not a raised triangle) and ch6 dawn shows the sail furled on the boom with the rope slack; the hull stays. Harbour-night keeps rope, mole, moon and cargo; boat-weather keeps the sail.
2. **harbour-night ↔ quiet-lantern**: both are night navy with a yellow lantern. Change **harbour-night**: replace the post lantern and its stepped rings with the **moon's soft halftone disc** (a single dot-size ramp, no steps) plus a **string of small paper quay lights** along the mole — no lantern object at all. Quiet-lantern keeps the lantern and its wedges.
3. **pocket-radio ↔ regulate ch3 (FEAR) ↔ loss ch2–3 ↔ unfinished-map ch1/2/6**: concentric navy/blue/white rings in four stories. Regulate keeps the arches (reference frame). Loss keeps its rings but they must stay *moving and off-centre* (the ch2 capture shows a perfectly centred static target — that frame is the problem: shift the impact point to (−220, −140) and let the outer rings leave the frame). **Pocket-radio**: break the rings into a **speaker grille** — each ring a dashed arc (segments 80–140 u with 30 u gaps that rotate slowly) so no frame shows solid concentric bands; keep solid rings only inside the dust cap r ≤ 220. **Unfinished-map**: contours must not be equidistant circles — make each hill an elongated loop (aspect 1.6–2.2, rotated 20–40°) with spacing that varies 40–90 u and two contours that merge.
4. **chalk-line ↔ futsl** (same path): both end/begin on the same green court with white markings. Change **futsl**: the hardwood floor becomes **cream paper with a pink .18 plank-grain halftone** (warm wood), court paint stays blue, walls stay pink .8, and only ch3 (the full-sized pitch) is green — so the wide pitch *contrasts* with the small court instead of matching it, and the story stops being seven chapters of one green.
5. **kite-turned ↔ reset**: stacked horizontal wind bands on paper, blue vs purple. Change **reset**: the purple bands become **curved streamlines that bend around the branch** (flow lines deflecting past an obstacle, compressing on the windward side), so the construction is "flow around a form", not "stacked bands"; raise band coverage to .35/.5/.7 so the story stops being the palest in the path.
6. **different-tides ↔ signal-water**: water in horizontal bands with a shore and a small paper ball. Distinct enough by colour (teal/blue sand vs green/orange/yellow) — no construction change, but the two balls must differ (fix 1) and different-tides' gauge post must not read as a flag pole (keep it as a ladder of marks, which it is).
7. **empathy ↔ itself**: ch1, ch3, ch5 and ch6 are the same two discs (green left, pink right, white dot) at the same scale; ch2 is the same at 2×. This is the one story that is repetitive *inside* itself. Fix 5.
8. **grit ch7 ↔ quiet-lantern ch3 ↔ loss ch4 ↔ regulate ch8**: a football alone on a navy field. Once the ball is redrawn per story (fix 1) this pair dissolves.

## 3. Does it read as one print run?

Yes, mostly: the same cream paper, the same navy key, the same halftone screen family, the same cream Knewave headline with the yellow rule, the same tray and title/Done button in every capture. The headline treatment is identical across all 18 (misregistered echo visible everywhere). Track-mode stories (futsl, grit, regulate) show a seek slider and "Continue" where the others show chapter dots — consistent per mode.

Against the three reference frames (whole-frame ink fields, fine speckle in the ink, torn edges, one huge motif, small confetti), the outliers:

- **Too pale / too flat (below reference texture level):**
  - **empathy** — the whole story. Discs never reach .7 coverage, edges are a blur ramp, there are no torn edges or confetti, the frame is 60 % bare pink-tinted paper. It reads as a soft-focus screen, not a print. The only story a viewer would call "digital".
  - **unfinished-map ch3** — a pencilled frame, two markers and a red triangle on bare cream; well under 60 % of the safe region. The paper-first look is this story's identity and is welcome as the one "paper" story, but ch3 needs its river band and terrain tone in frame.
  - **reset ch3–ch4** — cream with pale yellow and thin purple bands; ch4 is the palest frame in the set after empathy.
  - **kite-turned** — the green horizon field is a large even green with only fine speckle: the flattest big field in the set. Give it two stepped tonal bands (a darker torn band along the horizon and a lighter wear band) so it steps like the reference arches step.
  - **boat-weather ch1/ch3/ch4** — large paper-white sky and sail areas. Acceptable in ch2/ch3 where the cloth is the point; in ch4 the blue field with white lines is thin — add the rain-streak screen at .3 across it.
- **Too dark:** none is *wrong*, but seven stories carry a navy-night beat (list in §1a). Quiet-lantern (all six) and harbour-night (five) are the heavy ones; harbour-night ch3 (the deck) is the darkest frame in the set with the least paper showing — lift the deck to teal .5 with paper planks.
- **Digital-looking (too regular):**
  - **woven-court** — the plaid is a perfectly regular halftone lattice at what looks like .45–.5 coverage in every chapter (spec says .18/.26) with no rest area; on a phone it vibrates. ch2 is a frame of nothing but vertical stripes. Bring the lattice down to the spec'd coverage, wobble the band edges (`wob` amp 8), and leave a paper zone around the ball.
  - **futsl** — seven chapters of a perfectly regular stripe screen (the planks). Fix 4 handles it.
- **Halftone pitch (engine, not per story):** the coarse dot screens (chalk-line, different-tides ch1, loss ch2, harbour-night ch3) are visibly coarser than the reference's fine speckle at 390 px and moiré at the tray edge. This is consistent across the run so it does not break the set, but the engine agent should try a 25 % finer dot pitch at DPR ≤ 1.5, or speckle grain instead of dots above .6 coverage. Not a story fix.

## 4. Headline audit (§4c)

Rules: ≤ 2 words; a key point, never a title, summary or caption of what is on screen; never covering the action; "many chapters show none".

- **Length:** every shipped headline is ≤ 2 words. "LOOK. TRY." and "ASK. LISTEN" are two words with punctuation — acceptable, but both change for other reasons (§1a).
- **Titles / summaries / captions (must change):**
  - chalk-line ch2 **CHALK** — the metaphor's name, not a point → none (the chalk line drawing itself is the idea).
  - chalk-line ch5 **FEEDBACK** — the chapter label → **INFORMATION** (the point: a mistake gives information).
  - regulate ch1 **TWO GAMES** — the film's title → none.
  - grit ch8 **THE FRUIT** — a noun caption of what is on screen → **EVERYBODY WANTS** (the point is the wanting) or none.
  - more-shirt ch2 **OUTLINE** — names the object on screen → **NOT A BOUNDARY** is three words → **NO BOUNDARY**.
  - different-tides ch3 **PROGRESS TOO** — borderline summary; it is the sentence's key phrase, keep.
  - regulate **FRUSTRATION / FEAR** — captions of the emotion on screen, but they are the user's reference frames; keep.
- **Too many per story:** regulate 8 of 9 chapters; chalk-line 5 of 6; boat-weather 5 of 6. Target ≤ 4 per six-chapter story. After the changes above and in §1a: chalk-line → TRY, PRESSURE, INFORMATION (3); boat-weather → ADJUST, CHOOSE, ONE ACTION, TOGETHER (4); regulate → FRUSTRATION, FEAR, CONTROL, NOTICE, BREATHE, KEEP GOING, PLAY THROUGH (7 — still high, but the track has nine visual chapters and the reference frames carried words; drop **KEEP GOING** if it must be six).
- **Headline covering the action (checked at 390×850):**
  - more-shirt ch2 **OUTLINE** sits on the collar of the outline it names (collar at y −440 is directly under the headline band). Lower the outline 140 u or delay the headline to 6.0.
  - different-tides ch3 **PROGRESS TOO** sits on the reflected sun's top; ch4 **ONE THING** runs into the gauge post, which continues up under the title. Move the sun centre down 120 u; cap the post at y −380.
  - woven-court ch3 **MOVE** and ch4 **NEXT JOB** both sit on the loom-frame goal/D at the top of the court. Shift the ch3/ch4 camera down 150 u so the goal clears the band.
  - harbour-night ch2 **NOT WEAKNESS** sits on the lantern-ring glow (resolved by fix 2).
  - futsl: the captures at 0:17 and 0:31 show **TIGHTER** on the full-pitch chapter and **SCAN EARLY** on the web chapter. `headlineAt` returns '' for those captions, so this is the CSS leave animation still running after the capture script's seek; verify on device that a seek clears the outgoing headline immediately (it must not read "TIGHTER" over the wide pitch for any visible time).
- **Chapters with none:** every story has at least two silent chapters except regulate, chalk-line and boat-weather (fixed above).
