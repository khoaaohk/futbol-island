# loud-track — "The Loud Track" (7v7, a kinder inner voice)

Storyboard per RISO_BIBLE §8 with §1b (texture, uniqueness), §1c (kid legibility, abstract cut-paper figures), §4b (motion enacts the words), §4c (headlines) and §4d (delight). Written 21 Sep 2026 before drawing. Replaces "The Pocket Radio" (superseded storyboard in `superseded/pocket-radio.md`): kids do not listen to the radio; the metaphor is now **HEADPHONES** and a **loud track on repeat that you can SKIP**. Chapter data (narration, audio `/stories/narration/7v7/loud-track/{1..6}.m4a`, seconds `[10.1, 7.0, 7.6, 9.4, 9.3, 7.3]`, cue onsets) is the shipped stub and is not changed.

## Conventions

Short side 1080 u, chapter centre `(0,0)`, x right, y down; `cam(x,y,zoom)` puts world `(x,y)` at the centre of `sheet.safe`; zoom is multiplied by a viewport fit (`view()` ≤ 1.32 on phones, 1 on desktop) so the same composition fills every safe region. **Core box** `±400 × ±310` (the desktop safe region at zoom 1) holds everything that must read; backgrounds extend to `±2400`. Nothing important above `y = −330`. Cue `at` values are the shipped onsets. Every cue cell is **Ant. → Act. → Settle → Result** (§4b). Drawn objects on twos, camera on ones, all randomness seeded. Zoom keys are monotone inside a chapter (a single pull-back in ch 2 is monotone decreasing; every seam is a forward passage). Headline: HTML overlay, ≤ 2 words, only four chapters carry one.

## One world (side view)

The kid — **you** — is an abstract cut-paper figure (bible §1c.4: paper head disc, hand-cut paper torso, navy leg and arm strokes, navy contour, no face) standing on a **7v7 pitch** seen from the side: ground line `y = 230`, horizon `y = −30`. Above the pitch, the top of the frame is the **sound sky**: a whole-width ink field whose lower edge *is the track that is playing* — a **jagged orange edge with pink spikes** when the loud track plays, a **smooth yellow wave edge** when the kinder track plays. The kid wears big **navy headphones** (band over the head, two cups with paper faces). The inner voice is a **waveform arch** pouring out of one cup, over the head and back into the other cup — on repeat (it scrolls) — jagged orange with pink spikes (loud) or smooth yellow (kind). A big **paper music player** (navy hand-cut body with a pink misregistered shadow, a paper screen showing the playing wave, a big paper SKIP button with navy `|▶▶` marks, a small plain paper button) floats beside the kid like a thought (two paper thought discs from the headphones) and a paper **thumb** presses SKIP. Teammates/friends are **yellow figures**; the ball is a **paper football with navy pentagons** (orange shadow crescent).

## Background construction: the sound sky over a paper pitch

Every chapter: **top field = the playing track.** A yellow grainy halftone field (.6) with a solid yellow rim band (70 u) along its lower edge, the edge a slow smooth wave that scrolls; when the loud track plays an **orange field (.75, solid rim band) descends over it with a hand-cut jagged sawtooth edge** (segments 110 u, random peak heights, scrolling on twos) carrying **pink spike triangles** on the peaks; paper confetti fragments float in the field. **Bottom = the pitch:** cream paper with a navy .2 halftone, torn horizontal mown bands at .32, a darker torn horizon band at .45, paper touchline and box line knocked out. Chapter 2 is the one dark beat: inside the cup, a navy .32 field with a torn .6 band, the orange wave knocked out and printed clean over it, paper frames. How it differs from every other story: the field's *edge* is the animated subject (a waveform), not stacked bands (kite-turned, reset, signal-water, different-tides), not torn plates (place-picture), not washes (empathy), not rings (loss, superseded pocket-radio), not arches/channel/ribbons (regulate).

## Delight (§4d)

**Touch — a SKIP click.** `touch(sheet,x,y,age)`: a paper button (r 80, navy ring, navy `|▶▶` marks) stamps at the tap (ease-out-back 0.2 s), squashes at 0.2–0.3 s, four click lines burst, and a short **yellow smooth-wave blip** (300 u, amplitude 40) runs off to the right and fades by 0.8 s. Reduced motion: the stamped button only. Sized to pass the ≥ 2 % pixel gate at 320×568.

**Secondary motion:** confetti in the sky field scatters on spike beats; camera shakes 3–4 px on spikes and impacts; residual sway between cues; the player bobs; the arch scrolls always.

**End card:** the last composition holds; the yellow arch pulses once and the ball bumps once.
