## Football truth check

1. A ball kept at the feet with repeated inside-foot taps is futsal's first habit; the wall arrival shows "small court" literally (walls, not lines). 2. Flick = the ball lifted with the toe; sole roll = the sole on top, the ball dragged sideways; the turn takes the ball with the player; the wall pass returns off a real wall. 3. A defender blocking one side; the sole roll back and the exit on the other side is a real futsal solution (change of direction), not a trick. 4. Touches are counted per contact; the mistake is an over-hit touch off the wall; the next touch is softer. 5. Sole roll, outside-of-the-boot pass and a body fake are the three classic futsal deceptions, each beating a defender who commits the wrong way. 6. On the big pitch the same ideas appear as a pass, a flick and celebration.

## Touch micro-interaction
