**Inks** (print order): yellow `#ffe800` → fluorescent pink `#ff48b0` → green `#00a95c` → navy `#22366b` on cream `#f0ece2`. Screen angles 14° / 76° / 0° / 45°. No blue plate (woven-court owns pink/blue/yellow, kite-turned blue/yellow/green, futsl pink/blue/green).

**Role colours.** Court surface = green field (speckle, mottle, stepped navy vignette bands, yellow halftone wear patches). **Chalk = paper knockout** (the lead material: every mark, the ball, the arrows, the eye, the erased mistake). Chalk dust = paper speckle. Pressure / opponent = pink hand-cut masses that **knock the court out beneath them** (pink × green would print muddy, so pink always sits on paper). Support / tried options = yellow (over green it prints a yellow-green; over pink it prints orange — chapter 5). Key marks = navy (the chalk stick's contour, the pupil, chapter 5's masses: navy × pink = deep purple).

**Six views of one world (art-director rework).** Each chapter changes composition, scale, colour weight and the construction of the court behind it: **1** macro (zoom 1.25→1.6) on chalk dust and one mark in a dark court corner (navy-dimmed green, touchlines meeting, a pink corner block beyond the line); **2** the mark growing into a branching route seen from far above (zoom .5→.58, both corner circles, a pink boundary block along the far touchline, dashed yellow options); **3** close (zoom 1.3→1.5) on a chalk boot and ball with a big pink pressure block entering from the right; **4** the eye large (380 u) with two pink walls pushing the circle into a squashed oval, flakes flying from the contacts; **5** the purple/pink feedback print; **6** the whole branching route seen small on the full court (zoom .48→.54), pink blocks at both edges receding as the composition breathes out. Every chapter carries all three inks with weight: yellow halftone wear patches (cov .3–.5) and yellow options, pink masses/blocks (knocked out beneath), chalk flakes (paper + yellow fragments) that scatter from every push, orange where yellow meets pink at contact moments. The ball is a chalk circle with a wobbly double line and a dust halo.

**Background construction (unique to this story): a chalk court surface.** Whole-sheet green ink with speckle and mottle; stepped navy vignette bands (4 flat halftone steps toward the edges); court markings as huge chalk lines (centre circle r≈520 units, halfway line, corner arcs) with a lighter stepped zone inside the circle; chalk dust drifting on the surface. Chapter 5 is the story's one duotone beat: the same court markings printed on a **pink** field (inside the pressure ink) with navy masses. No arches, no torn channel, no diagonal ribbons (those belong to other stories).

**Lead imagery.** A stick of chalk (paper body, navy contour, pink halftone shadow), the chalk line that grows and branches, a chalk-drawn circle standing in for the ball, chalk arrows for options, a chalk-sketched boot sole for the touch, one chalk-sketched eye (chapter 4 only), pink pressure masses, yellow support lane. No bubble, no glove, no faces.

## Football truth check

1. Hesitation before beginning is shown as a hovering chalk, not a player freezing. 2. A line changes direction one mark at a time — exploring without the whole picture. 3. First touch: the ball arrives, is met, and moves to a new angle in one contact; settle after. 4. Pressure closes toward the ball; support offers a diagonal angle; the pass is short (0.7 s) and the receiver cushions. 5. A pass into a closing space fails; the information is the closing space and the touch size; the next attempt is shaped smaller and into an opened lane. 6. Trying ideas leaves some marks that fizzle; the useful one grows.
