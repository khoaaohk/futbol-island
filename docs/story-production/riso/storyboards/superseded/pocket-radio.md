## Lead imagery (this story only)

**Radio dial, speaker cone, waveform, tuning needle.** The **speaker cone** = concentric wave rings radiating from a navy dust cap; the **dial** = a wide navy arc with an orange station band and a pink station band, tick marks, a **needle** and a yellow dial light; the **waveform** = a thread across the frame whose bump motif is redrawn per state (jagged / rounded / flat / kinked); **wave-packets** (a three-bump piece of waveform that travels along the thread — the words a station sends); **station nodes** (paper knockout dots r=40 with a navy ring on the thread — you = pink-filled, teammate = yellow-filled); **orange static** (speckle confetti creeping in from an edge). Football objects only where the narration names a football action, in radio material: the ball is a **pulse** (a rounded bump on the thread with a paper crest dot); a pass = the pulse travelling the thread from one node to the next; a mistake = the pulse jumping off the thread and falling. Generic marks at most once: the eye once (ch 5, the dial's paper **lens window** with a navy pupil). No boot, no hand, no ear, no bubble, no figures.

*Lead imagery not used by any other story in this path* (place-picture: pigment plates/slot/offcuts; signal-water: shores/signal and returning ripples/flag/buoy; empathy: soft translucent washes/wash ear/wash hand).

## Background construction: concentric speaker-wave rings

Whole story: **5–6 full concentric rings** of navy at stepped coverage (0.25 → 0.75 toward the origin), each a **grainy ink band** with a wobbly torn edge, printed over a **grainy yellow field** (navy×yellow overprint darkens each ring's inner step to olive-navy). The origin is the dust cap and **moves per chapter** (top-centre, inside the cone, the dial hub, far top-left, far above, top-left again). The rings **deform with the waveform**: jagged and shaking when the harsh station plays, smooth when the kind station plays, a flat line cutting through them when breathing. **Orange static speckle** is the confetti when noise returns. No arches, no channel, no ribbons.

**How it differs from the other 7v7 stories:** full circles from an off-centre acoustic source that change shape with the cues — the background is the sound. Place-picture's background is hard torn plates; signal-water's is horizontal water bands between two torn shorelines; empathy's is soft translucent washes. It differs from regulate's reserved nested arches by being full rings, off-centre with a moving origin, deforming with the waveform, and printed over yellow with an olive overprint step.

## Delight (§4d)

**Touch reaction — dial click + waveform blip.** `touch(sheet, x, y, age)`: the needle **jitters 2°** with a paper tick knockout printed at the nearest dial tick (or, when the dial is off-frame, the nearest ring prints a tick), and a small rounded **blip** (a 60-u bump) is born on the nearest thread at the touch's x, travelling 120 u along it and fading (0.6 s, ease-out); the nearest ring steps outward +8 u and back (0.3 s). Ink: pink blip on a pink/navy thread, orange blip if the touch lands in static. Reduced motion: one static tick + bump. ≤ 6 live.

**Secondary motion (seeded, bounded):** orange splatter specks fly off the waveform when it turns jagged or stamps (gravity, 0.5 s); the rings' residual quiver after a shake decays over 0.5 s; static speckles scatter from the pulse's fall and from the eraser's drop; paper click knockouts blink at ticks the needle passes; the dial light flickers once on each detent; a 1-frame double contour when a node "rings"; the thread slackens/tightens behind travelling packets; a 4-px slap shiver on each stamp.

**End card:** the last print holds; the needle does one 1° settle onto pink and the pulse's crest dot re-prints in register.

## What would make this fail

- A small literal radio in the middle of the frame with a wiggling cable (the rejected version). Here the cone is 2200 u across and cropped; the dial is a 1500-u arc; the waveform and rings cross the whole frame.
- The waveform or rings wiggling constantly: they shake only on noise cues and hold between, with a decaying quiver.
- A flat background: rings are stepped grainy bands over a grainy yellow field with olive overprint; the duotone chapter is orange + navy tone on paper, never a black screen.
- Words drawn in the artwork; a headline that captions ("The noisy radio") instead of the one word to keep.
- Illustrating beside the words: a "noisy" cue without shake and splatter; a "breathe" cue where nothing expands; a "look up" without the camera panning as the look.
- Reusing another story's imagery: no boot, hand, ear, bubble, pigment plates, water or washes. The eye appears once, as the lens window.
- Unrelated symbols (notes, headphones, bolts); tiny icon grids; full-body figures or faces.
- Reverse zoom beyond the one 4 % breathe-out; hard cuts; flat holds > 1.5 s; idle wobble.
