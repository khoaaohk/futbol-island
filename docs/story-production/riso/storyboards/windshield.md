## 0. Print setup

- **Paper** `#f0ece2`. **Inks (3 + navy):** yellow `#ffe800`, orange `#ff6c2f`, blue `#0078bf`, navy `#22366b` (last). Print order yellow → orange → blue → navy. Same triple as regulate/loss (unavoidable in 11v11) but a different **dominance**: this is a **night-blue print** (blue field + navy screen = the night sky and the pitch seen through the pane), **yellow = light** (the headlight fan on the road, the found-space pool, the marker rings), **orange = pressure** (the surprise defender, the pressing mass, road-edge posts). Overprints used on purpose: yellow × blue = the green of the lit pitch (the headlights turn the road into grass); orange × blue = the brown shadow under the pressing mass.
- **Background construction (unique to this story): "the windshield pane over a perspective night road-pitch".** A dark car cabin is the frame — two hand-cut navy A-pillars leaning inward, a torn navy header with a small rear-view mirror, a navy dashboard with a paper-and-blue lip — and through the wide pane a **perspective road** (converging edges to a vanishing point) that *is* the pitch (paper touchlines, halfway line, foreshortened centre circle, a far goal with two posts, bar and net), lit by a **stepped yellow headlight fan** (3 torn bands, near .75 → far .3). The pane carries **rain streaks** (blue quads running down the glass) and two **wiper blades** whose sweeps clear arc-shaped clean sectors. Ch1 is the only chapter without the pane (a stadium seen from behind a player, floodlight beam + crowd band) so the pane's arrival in ch2 is the reveal. Nothing in the set uses a framed pane, a perspective road, wipers or rain-on-glass: quiet-lantern = radial wedges in a dark room; boat-weather = torn fronts + rain streaks *in the air* + sailcloth; more-shirt = knit rows; regulate = the three reference constructions; reset = flow bands; loss = pond ripples. The navy-night beat is shared with several stories but here the night is always *inside a frame* and cut by the road wedge — it never reads as a dark room.
- **Lead imagery:** the windshield (pillars, dashboard, mirror, wipers, rain), the road that becomes a pitch, the headlight fan / headlight pool, the tunnel spot (a stepped navy vignette closing to one circle), yellow marker rings ("found early"), a wiper used as a clock hand that slows. **Generic shapes:** one eye (ch6 only), drawn in this story's material: a paper almond whose iris is a small round windshield (blue night disc, yellow headlight fan, a tiny wiper); no ear, no hand, no bubble. **Football objects in this story's material:** the ball = **the headlight ball**: paper sphere with a yellow lit crescent on its near side, one navy centre pentagon and five short navy tread-seams (no outer pentagons), navy contour, a paper glint, and a yellow pool under it on the road; a boot (ch5 kicking foot, ch6 huge cropped boot: navy wedge, paper lace ticks, orange sole stripe); the far goal (paper posts + bar, blue-tone net); passes = the ball travelling 0.65–0.8 s player to player with a cushion.
