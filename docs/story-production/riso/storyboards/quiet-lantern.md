## 0. Print setup

- **Paper** `#f0ece2`. **Inks (3 + navy):** purple `#765ba7`, yellow `#ffe800`, blue `#0078bf`, navy `#22366b` (last). Print order: yellow → blue → purple → navy. Registration 1.8 px re-seeded per chapter; ink alpha .9; coverage cap .8.
- **This is a NIGHT print.** Every background is a heavy **blue × purple overprint field** (blue .55 × purple .4 → indigo) with dense speckle grain (density .5 tile) and low-frequency mottle .25, paper showing through everywhere. Never a flat dark fill. Light is a *reduction* of that field (knockout ramp) plus **yellow halftone**.
- **Background construction (unique to this story): "a dark room pierced by one lantern's stepped light cone."** From each lit lantern a cone of 5 wedges (half-angle 28–32°, lengths stepping ~160 u apart), each wedge one flat print of yellow tone at stepped coverage (.62 / .45 / .32 / .20 / .10) with hand-cut wobbly edges (wob amp 6), and inside each wedge the indigo field is knocked down by the same step so paper shows. Grain stays visible inside the light (it is a print, not a glow filter). Dust motes = small paper specks only inside wedges 1–2. Later chapters multiply the cones (two side by side, then eleven) and lay one down flat as a lit lane. Stepped **radial wedges from a point**, never concentric bands.
- **How it differs from the other 11v11 stories:** regulate owns nested concentric arches, the torn channel and diagonal ribbons; boat-weather owns weather fronts, rain streaks and sailcloth; more-shirt owns knit rows and a growing outline; reset (legacy) uses a bending branch in wind bands; loss (legacy) uses settling water ripples. Nothing here is concentric, striped, woven or rippled: it is a dark room / night pitch cut by radial stepped wedges of light.
- **Lead imagery:** a lantern; its stepped light cone; a path revealed (stepping marks lit one at a time); a second lantern lit by the first. **Lead imagery not used by any other story in this path.** Contrast object (ch1 only): a floodlight mast (the loud voice). Cross-path note: harbour-night (9v9) draws a harbour lantern as concentric stepped *rings* on still water; here the light is a **directional stepped cone** (wedges from a point, never rings) that scans, squeezes to a beam and lies down as a lane — a different construction and a different verb set.
- **Generic shapes:** no eye, no ear, no hand, no speech bubble anywhere. Looking = the camera pans *as* the cone swings; a message = the cone narrowed to a beam; listening = my cone turned down while I stand in another's light.
- **Football objects only when named:** the ball appears in ch3 ("look beyond the ball"), ch4 ("passing option"), ch5 (the mistake: a misplaced pass rolling into the dark), ch6 ("helpful action" pass). Always a **lantern-lit sphere**: a paper disc whose navy panels print only on the lit side and fade into the field on the dark side.
- **Headlines (§4c):** HTML overlay in `IslandLoadingBrush`, cream with navy echo; one or two words, only where a chapter has one idea to keep: ch1 none · ch2 **NEXT STEP** (arrives at 4.66 with the lantern's tilt, leaves at the seam) · ch3 **LOOK BEYOND** (arrives at 0.00 as the beam lifts) · ch4 **USEFUL** (arrives at 0.00 as the cone squeezes to a beam) · ch5 **LISTEN** (arrives at 0.00 as my cone retracts) · ch6 none. Nothing lettered on canvas.
- **World units:** short side = 1080 u; chapter centre (0,0); +x right, +y down. Camera key = (cx, cy, zoom[, rot°]); zoom 1 shows 1080 u across the short side. **Core box K** = x −470..470, y −400..400 holds everything important (inside `safe` above the tray and below the title at 390×850, 320×568, 844×390, 1440×850). Bleed: portrait to y ±820, landscape to x ±860 — backgrounds and repeats only. Zoom never decreases inside a chapter or across a seam. Drawn objects on twos; camera, light and passages every frame.
- **Motion grammar (§4b) used throughout:** every cue = anticipation (0.15–0.3 s counter-move) → action with a real ease → overshoot/settle → consequence on something else; the camera leans into pushes, is dragged late by fast swings, and *is* the look on "notice/look". Masses: the floodlight head is heavy (slow start, hard stop); lantern flames are light (flutter); cones have no mass but their *edges* overshoot 4° and settle. Residual sway: a lantern on a hook swings and decays (freq .8 Hz, decay 2 s) after every action that touches it.
- **Role colours (fixed):** ball = paper with navy panels (lit side only); pressure / opponent = **purple solid .9 "shadows"** (long torn wedges lying on the field); support / teammate = **an unlit lantern body (navy contour, field glass) that knocks out to paper when lit**; metaphor material = **yellow halftone light** (cone wedges) and **lantern glass** (paper knockout + yellow tone .5). Pitch lines = paper knockout, readable only where light falls (ghost .15 elsewhere).

## 2. Dominant material metaphor and its development

**Lantern light as a stepped print.** Light is never a soft glow: it is a cone of stepped yellow halftone wedges that *reduce* the indigo field so the paper — and whatever is printed on it — can be read. Development: ch1 the loud floodlight (flat wedge, no steps, flattens and shoves everything) versus the small stepped cone (reads the bench and the path) → ch2 the cone's steps show one stepping mark at a time; trying to fill the room flattens everything → ch3 the cone becomes a scanning beam that lifts off the ball and marks three needs with navy contours while the camera pans as the look → ch4 the beam squeezes to a message (a name, a danger, a lane laid flat) and three cones travel and join into shared light → ch5 my cone retracts so I stand in a teammate's light, then reaches over to re-light a guttering lantern (second lantern lit) → ch6 eleven lanterns, a chain of cones joining across the whole pitch to the far goal. The same wedge construction at four scales (corner of a room → floor → half pitch → whole pitch); the stepping-mark path from ch2 returns in ch5 and at pitch length in ch6.

## 3. Ink choice and role colours

Purple + yellow + blue + navy (unique triple in the 11v11 path; boat-weather = purple/orange/blue, more-shirt = purple/orange/yellow, regulate = orange/yellow/blue). Night field = blue × purple overprint (indigo) with grain. Light = yellow halftone steps + knockout. Ball = paper with navy panels on the lit side. Pressure/opponents = purple .9 solid shadows lying on the field (they flinch, get pushed, crumple). Support/teammates = lantern bodies (navy contour) that knock out to paper when lit. Shared light = yellow × yellow overlaps (.75). Duotone beat: optional in ch2 (blue + yellow only) for "a small light in a dark space".

## 5. What would make this fail (Astra-version symptoms to avoid)

- A flat dark background: the night must be blue × purple halftone with grain and mottle, paper visible through it.
- A lantern as a small centred icon with a radial-gradient glow; the cone must be stepped wedges and huge (wedge 5 exceeds the frame).
- Holding a composition while the narration continues (any hold > 1.5 s without a cue-driven change), or motion that merely fades things in next to the words instead of pushing, squeezing, reaching, joining.
- Speech bubbles, eyes, ears or hands for "call", "look", "listen" — none here; the cone and the camera do all of it.
- A grid of little icons for "teammate / runner / left out": they are three places on one continuous pitch that the beam visits.
- Full-body players or faces: teammates are lantern bodies; opponents are shadows on the ground.
- A zoom-in-then-out at any seam or inside a chapter; every seam is a forward passage through named glass or overlap light.
- Any lettering on the canvas; headlines are HTML, one or two words, only where listed.

## 6. Delight (§4d)

- **Touch reaction `touch(sheet,x,y,age)` ≤ 0.8 s:** a **lantern flare**. From the nearest lit lantern (or the tap point itself if no lantern is lit) a 3-wedge mini cone (half-angle 20°, lengths 90/160/230, coverages .6/.35/.15) blooms toward the tap point with overshoot (0.25 s) and decays (0.55 s); if the point is within 80 u of a stepping mark or pitch line, that mark knocks out to paper for the flare's life and gets a 1-frame navy contour tick; 6 dust motes scatter from the point and settle. Yellow + knockout only; navy tick. Reduced motion: a single static flare wedge for 0.4 s.
- **Secondary motion that reacts to main actions:** dust motes inside wedges 1–2 (scatter when a cone snaps or a blast hits, drift back after); the flame teardrop (flutters on every cone move, gutters on the mistake); lantern hook sway (residual, decays 2 s, restarted by every push); shadow tips (flinch 20 u when a beam approaches, crumple when pushed); the floodlight's speed lines (re-seeded per pulse); the torn edge of the floor band (crumples under the blast, relaxes after).
- **Headline arrival:** NEXT STEP / LOOK BEYOND / USEFUL / LISTEN pop in with the misregistration settle (CSS) at chapter start; none in ch1 and ch6.
- **End card:** the eleven flames flicker-settle together once; Replay stamp.
