## Lead imagery (this story only)

**Two shores, a signal ripple, a returning ripple, a flag/marker.** The **near shore** (yellow sand, torn shoreline at the bottom) and the **far shore** (orange sand, torn shoreline at the top) with **water bands** between; the **you-flag** (a yellow pennant on a navy pole on the near shore) and the **helper flags** (orange pennants on the far shore); the **signal ripple** = yellow concentric arcs travelling out from the you-flag; the **ask-ripple** = a signal whose top arc lifts into a curl with a paper dot beneath (a question drawn as water); the **returning ripple** = orange arcs coming back from a helper flag; the **interference lattice** = where yellow and orange arcs overprint (deep-orange crossings) — a bridge of ripples; **fog** = a navy grainy field on the water; **glints** = paper chips on the water as confetti. Football objects only where the narration names a football action, in water material: the ball is a **buoy** (paper disc r=50 with a navy band, floating, leaving a wake); a pass = the buoy pushed across the water; a pitch = paper-knockout lines on the surface; a player's move = the flag relocating along a dashed paper lane. Generic marks at most once: the ear once (ch 5, a **shell** — returning arcs curling into a crescent). No boot, no hand, no eye, no bubble, no figures.

*Lead imagery not used by any other story in this path* (place-picture: pigment plates/slot/offcuts; pocket-radio: cone/dial/needle/waveform; empathy: soft translucent washes/wash ear/wash hand).

## Background construction: water ripple bands between two shores

Whole story: **5–6 horizontal water bands** of green at stepped coverage (0.3 at the far shore → 0.7 at the near shore = depth), each a **grainy ink field** whose upper edge is a gentle wave line (amplitude 18 u, wavelength 420 u, wobbled), over cream paper; the **near shore** a yellow grainy field with a torn shoreline (bottom) and the **far shore** an orange grainy field with a torn shoreline (top); where a band meets a shore the overprint makes an olive (green×yellow) or brown (green×orange) tide-line step; **paper glints** (~30 u) float on the water. The band amplitude is the water's mood: dead flat when silent, alive when a signal passes, sagging under fog, stretched by a current. **Fog** (navy halftone 0.6 with torn holes) lies on the bands in ch 2. No arches, no channel, no ribbons.

**How it differs from the other 7v7 stories:** horizontal wave bands between two torn shorelines, read as depth and distance, that respond as a body of water. Place-picture's background is hard registered plates; pocket-radio's is concentric rings from a speaker; empathy's is soft-edged translucent washes with no torn edges.
