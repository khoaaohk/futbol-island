## Conventions

Short side 1080 u, x right, y down. **One house world** shared by every chapter (the camera travels through it): ground line `y = 320`; basement interior `x ±420, y 320..620` (floor slab 620..660, walls ±420..±484); foundation courses `y 240..320` across `±484`; first floor interior `y −40..240`; slab `−80..−40`; second floor `−320..−80`; ceiling slab `−360..−320`; roof eaves `(±524, −360)`, apex `(0, −680)`. `cam(x,y,zoom)`; zoom never decreases inside a chapter; objects on twos, camera on ones; every random value seeded; seam = last 0.65 s; headline = HTML overlay, nothing lettered on canvas (ages are shown by the figure's growth, never digits).

## Lead imagery (this story only)

**A cut-paper cross-section house that grows floor by floor, and the player who grows with it.** Bricks (orange hand-cut blocks with paper mortar) are the material: a brick pile, bricks flying into courses and *stamping* in with a dust puff, a brick missing → cracks and wobble, the brick stamped back → settle. Rooms lit by yellow light. Floor slabs and a beam (lifted overhead = strength). A door-frame height mark (growth). A big back-wall window whose shutters open onto a mini pitch (reading the game). A roof of rafters + tile rows, placed at night; a far-off disco box with flashing windows; the Saturday sun rising; the ridge tie beam that holds the house (commitment). **Figures** (§1c.4): abstract cut-paper pictograms (disc head, hand-cut torso, two leg strokes, two arm strokes): *you* = yellow, grows kid → teen → older teen → finished player (h 210 → 265 → 355 → 340 → 390 → 420), *teammate* = orange, *opponent* = navy. **The football** = the *brick-dust ball*: paper disc, five hand-cut **orange** pentagons in a net with navy seams, an orange halftone shadow crescent and a navy contour (no other story prints orange panels). Generic marks: none (no eye/ear/hand/bubble).

Not used by the other 7v7 stories (place-picture: pigment plates/figures in a picture; pocket-radio: cone/dial/waveform; signal-water: shores/flags/arcs/buoy; empathy: washes).

## Background construction: a hillside cross-section with the house as the huge central motif

The sky is a **yellow field with two stepped brighter torn bands toward the top** (light = yellow), the hill is a **green turf ribbon along a sloping ground profile** (rising to the left, falling to the right, a flat cut where the house sits), and below the profile **four warm orange soil strata** (.35 → .8, torn upper edges following the slope, orange × yellow overprint = deep brick orange, sparse darker pebble chips). Cut into the hill and rising above it, the **house cross-section** fills ≥ 60 % of the safe region in every chapter: brick walls (two flat orange prints alternating brick by brick, paper mortar), yellow-lit rooms, orange slabs with navy contours, a tiled roof. Chapters move through the same world: ch1 the site and the dug basement, ch2 inside the soil at the foundation line, ch3 the first floor at ground level, ch4 *inside* the second-floor room (yellow plaster wall, the window opening onto a green pitch), ch5 the roof at night (navy night field knocked out around the warm house; disco left, sunrise right), ch6 the whole house on the hill, then its cutaway.

How it differs: grit (9v9) uses horizontal navy/blue underground strata pierced by roots; here the strata are warm orange, sloped as a hill, only a base for the house, and the dominant construction is the brick house cutaway (no other story has brick coursework, rooms, slabs or a roof). Signal-water (same triple) is green-dominant water bands with yellow/orange shores; this story is orange-brick + yellow-light dominant with green only as turf and the window pitch.
