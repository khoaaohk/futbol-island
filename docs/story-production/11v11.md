# 11v11 story production

Three original six-beat films, written for older youth (roughly 12+); playing format is not an age definition. Each lasts approximately 60 seconds and connects a metaphor to an actionable football situation.

- **The Quiet Lantern**: practical leadership through checking on a teammate, scanning, and one clear defensive message. A lantern's light spreads into a connected team.
- **The Boat and the Weather**: accepting frustration while choosing recovery position, scanning and support. A changing sail opens into a field of available actions; a comeback is not promised.
- **More Than a Shirt**: ask about a changed role, carry strengths into it, and retain relationships/interests outside football. A shirt opens into a person surrounded by a ball, book and pencil.

Original Canvas geometry uses continuous eased transformations and reveals across beat boundaries. Compositions scale to the available width and height. Reduced motion fixes progress at a meaningful midpoint for each beat and disables time motion. The shared player supplies grain, captions, audio lifecycle and controls. These draw functions create no timers, DOM elements, textures, audio contexts or animation loops. Fixed small shape counts bound runtime work; no filters or shadows. These are added active playback costs, not evidence of reduced phone heat.

Narration is **temporary synthesized macOS Samantha**, generated at 145 words/minute, encoded to AAC M4A with installed ffmpeg. Each clip is measured with afinfo; per-story timing.json records actual clip durations and padded beat timings. Every beat exceeds its clip duration by at least 0.4 seconds. Files use 01.m4a through 06.m4a.
