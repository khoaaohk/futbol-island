# Individual story rebuild, September 20, 2026

The user rejected the September 19 transition-only rollout. Its successful technical checks did not establish that the artwork met the requested style. ## Design contract

Large, legible, textured painted forms with a deliberate foreground/background relationship. Cropped boots, the original cream gloves with blue palms and gold cuffs, filled speech bubbles, the actual five-panel football construction, pressure walls and broad material close-ups belong to the same visual family. Do not use full-body figures, miniature scenic worlds, blurry raster artwork, tiny equal-sized icon grids or arbitrary flashing to communicate an idea.

Each paragraph has an authored composition; changes inside it follow the spoken clauses. A material passage must enter a different composition. Other boundaries use a short straight material reveal without a reverse zoom. Keep foreground silhouettes visible while crossing background colors. A seam passing a pixel test is not a visual signoff.

## Love Futsal: individual audit and replacement

The previous film used animated background loops, floating dots, little pitch diagrams, decorative arrows and a different cyan-heavy palette. Several scenes repeated a layout or moved without explaining the spoken action.

1. **0–5.36s:** a broad blue court narrows around a chalk connection; the ball grows into the focal object as the game’s possibilities are introduced.
2. **5.36–10.7s:** orange/pink pressure materials move inward, then the ball arrives quickly; supporting spaces become closer afterward.
3. **10.7–16.56s:** a cropped clock hand spends the available time; three straight choices grow from its base and the ball chooses one.
4. **16.56–23.3s:** generous negative space surrounds a controlled touch. A cropped eye scans, and the available time closes into a thin strip at “moments.”
5. **23.3–26.84s:** a large eye scans first; only afterward does the boot/ball arrive for the tight-space touch.
6. **26.84–31.04s:** an orange obstacle blocks the direct route. A pass to support followed by a return from a new angle demonstrates the solution.
7. **31.04–38.2s:** two connected role materials attack, recover goal-side, offer an angle and look. The goal supplies a persistent reference.
8. **38.2–46.26s:** the court material opens behind a deliberate touch and a chosen passing lane. The ball and boot have real contact rather than floating apart.
9. **46.26s–end:** the learned passing geometry extends beyond the small court’s original bounds into a broad field.

Visual review corrected boot/ball contact, removed the eye before “think,” and retained actual painted material anchors. Narration and caption timing are unchanged.

## Grit: individual audit and replacement

The previous film relied on a literal tree, skyline, stadium, tiny player, particles and extreme camera moves. That made the story visually inconsistent with the approved abstract sequences. The replacement keeps the root/fruit metaphor but uses large sections of material and purposeful growth.

1. **0–13.06s:** a seed sends roots downward, then a stem upward; branching fruit mirrors the established roots.
2. **13.06–23.5s:** a macro seed responds to gravity below and a cropped golden light field above. Roots and the shoot have different directions and colors.
3. **23.5–27.18s:** the seed opens and establishes its downward root before upward growth.
4. **27.18–37.2s:** the root encounters a slab inside narrowing pressure walls, then grows continuously around it. Segments grow in order so the root never breaks apart mid-action.
5. **37.2–49.22s:** a large shoot emerges as ground panels separate. Leaves open toward the light; the established roots stay connected underneath.
6. **49.22–60.2s:** a cropped practice touch bears a heavy material above; its established root connects to later fruit. This ties unseen effort to a football action without a full-body player.
7. **60.2–67.14s:** the fruit becomes a large focal surface; hands reach for the visible outcome while its root connection is revealed.
8. **67.14s–end:** return to a controlled practice touch and a small rooted shoot. The ending depicts continuing effort rather than an instant stadium celebration.

Source animation remains on the original 76.375-second clock. Offline exports invert the saved Adam recording map for the actual 69.10-second media clock. Existing audio is copied and hash-checked, not regenerated.
