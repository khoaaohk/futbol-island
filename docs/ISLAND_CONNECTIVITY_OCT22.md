# Island path connectivity audit (Oct 22 2026)

Generated by `node scripts/audit-island-connectivity.cjs --md docs/ISLAND_CONNECTIVITY_OCT22.md` (pure Node, no browser).
Guarded by `tests/island-connectivity.cjs` (in `npm test`).

## Method

- Builds the real island in Node (`lib/town/world.ts` buildTown) plus the colliders Town.tsx adds at runtime (vending machines, fishing kiosks).
- Rasterises a 0.5 m grid over x -104..812, z -268..252 with the game's own tests: `groundBlocked` terrain (shoreline polygon, Coral Cay, causeway, sandbars, Summit Isle, landable decks, ferry ramp), every obstacle at the walking radius 0.32 m, `rooftopTravel` surface heights (buildings, walk surfaces, stairs, ride ramps, raised pitches).
- Flood-fills from the spawn (103, -8) with the walking rules of `rooftopTravel.update`: building-like colliders block only when more than 0.35 m above the walker, other colliders block on the ground, a step up is at most 0.35 m (stairs whose 0.45 m treads alias on the grid are checked at the midpoint), drops are allowed. Summit ground has no slope limit (as in `groundBlocked`). Swimming is not counted.
- A reverse flood finds one-way pockets (reached but no walk back). Landmarks count as reached when a reached cell is within their talk / trigger range.
