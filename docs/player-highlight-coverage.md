# Player highlight library — September 16, 2026

No API key or runtime YouTube searches. 527 saved clips across 135 of 196 players. 94 players have at least three options.

Official publisher identity, title, duration and views come from public YouTube search/watch metadata. `metadataSource: public-search` entries were not individually checked for regional/embed availability because YouTube returned HTTP 429 during watch-page verification. Playback errors use another saved clip of the same player. No claim that every clip is playable in the US or that publisher identity guarantees child suitability. View counts are snapshots, not live rankings.

News retains the 14-day limit; career highlights may be older. Prefer 4+ minute videos, sorted by views within that group; shorter official clips remain backups. No unrelated clips substitute for missing players.
