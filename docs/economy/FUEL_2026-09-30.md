# Fuel: "fuel your body like a player" (30 Sep 2026)

Local only. Nothing here is committed or deployed. This follows the [29 Sep economy update](ECONOMY_UPDATE_2026-09-29.md) and the [30 Sep learning-loop note](ECONOMY_UPDATE_2026-09-30.md) and keeps their goals: learning pays best, and a regular player owns everything in 60–90 days.

**User request:** "Add a fuel bar with the coins, fish and fruit. Basically you need fuel to do things around the island; you will need to go to the Konbini to buy items to replenish. Factor this into the economy. Research if we should give away money after completing a quiz so they can buy the items more easily." A follow-up added: "Flying spends more fuel since it's easier to do; walking the least."

**Short version:**
- Fuel is a 0–100 bar in the coins bar. Only optional fast travel uses it. Flying uses the most, then the moped, bike and scooter, then a Shift sprint. Walking uses almost none and **always works, even at 0**.
- Learning never costs fuel and is never blocked. Neither are jobs, fishing or talking.
- You refuel with Konbini food and drinks (including the drink machines) and with fruit and veg from the island pocket. The free refuel needs no coins at all:
  - **breakfast**: every new local day starts with a full tank (100);
  - **garden picking**: fruit and veg picked in the Community Garden are free, go into the pocket and can be eaten there;
  - **the Garden shift's share**: the paid Garden shift job also puts a small share of what you picked (2 / 1 / 1 items by pay tier) into the basket, like Harvest day's farmer's share.

  So a child with 0 coins can always refuel. (The six free water fountains described in earlier drafts were removed on 30 Sep 2026; see the update at the end.)
- **Eased for a kids' game (user, 30 Sep 2026):** drain rates were halved and then cut again (a tank ≈ 28 min of flying, 9 h of walking), climbing or steering on the jetpack counts as flying (a hands-off hover is free since 3 Oct), breakfast fills the tank, and food refuels about twice as much (one meal fills the tank). The fuel coin sink is now negligible (§4). Fuel stays mainly a nutrition lesson.
- Nothing drains while you are away, and there are no timers.
- **Quiz coins: no change.** Lessons already pay 12 coins (+6 for a perfect first try) once each. We should not add more coins per quiz, and certainly no repeatable quiz pay, to fund fuel. Fuel is cheap, and its free path is fruit, not coins.

## 1. Research

### 1.1 Energy/stamina systems in children's games: what the guidance says

| Source | What it says | What it means for us |
|---|---|---|
| ICO, *Age appropriate design code*, standard 5 "Detrimental use of data" ([ICO](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/childrens-information/childrens-code-guidance-and-resources/age-appropriate-design-a-code-of-practice-for-online-services/5-detrimental-use-of-data/); summaries: [ESRB](https://www.esrb.org/privacy-certified-blog/the-icos-age-appropriate-design-code-uses-detrimental-to-children/), [TIGA](https://tiga.org/news/ico-age-appropriate-design-code-tigas-summary)) | Avoid "sticky" features that extend engagement, such as reward loops, features that encourage continuous play, and notifications. Children should be able to take breaks **without losing progress** (pause). | No offline drain and no refill timer that brings a child back ("your energy is full!"). No notifications. A break never costs anything. |
| 5Rights Foundation, *Disrupted Childhood: the cost of persuasive design* (2018; updated 2023) ([Hertfordshire](https://researchprofiles.herts.ac.uk/en/publications/disrupted-childhood-the-cost-of-persuasive-design/), [5Rights](https://5rightsfoundation.com/five-key-changes-introduced-by-the-age-appropriate-design-code/)) | Persuasive design aimed at time-on-app (variable rewards, artificial scarcity, fear of missing out) harms children's development. | Fuel must not be a scarcity lever. You can always walk, and the zero-coin refill (fruit) is always nearby. |
| Radesky et al., *Prevalence and characteristics of manipulative design in mobile applications used by children*, JAMA Network Open 2022 ([JAMA](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamanetworkopen/fullarticle/2793493)) | 80% of apps used by 3–5-year-olds had manipulative design, mostly to prolong play or push purchases. Patterns included fabricated time pressure, navigation constraints, lures and parasocial pressure. | No time pressure ("refuel now!"). Running out of fuel never blocks navigation (walking). No character guilt-trips the player. Messages are calm and say what to do. |
| FTC, *Bringing Dark Patterns to Light* (staff report, Sept 2022) ([FTC PDF](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/P214800+Dark+Patterns+Report+9.14.2022+-+FINAL.pdf)) | Highlights children's apps where a "continue" button turns into "Buy", and enforcement over kids' in-app charges. | Fuel is bought only with in-game coins at a counter the child chooses to walk to. There is no "refuel" button on the empty notice, and no real money exists in the game. |
| Common Sense Media game ratings ("Consumerism") ([how they rate games](https://d8.commonsensemedia.org/about-us/our-mission/about-our-ratings/games)); the classic "energy meter" pattern ([TV Tropes, Freemium Timer](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/FreemiumTimer)) | Consumerism and in-game purchases are a rating concern. The typical energy meter forces a choice: **wait hours or pay**. | Ours is neither. It refills from food (in-game coins) or free fruit, and never by waiting or paying. |

**Conclusion.** An energy system is safe for children only if:
- it never gates the core activity (learning, here);
- it never uses time or absence against the child;
- it has a free, local way back;
- its messages are informative, not pressuring.

The design below follows all four. The Konbini's own 29 Sep decision ("no gameplay perk: a paid stamina boost would be pay-to-win") is honoured in spirit:
- Food never makes you faster or stronger than normal. It only restores the optional fast modes.
- A free source (fruit) restores the same thing.

### 1.2 Real sports nutrition for young players (the teaching)

- **Carbohydrate is football's main fuel.** FIFA's *Nutrition for Football* ([PDF](https://digitalhub.fifa.com/m/16e433eb11621446/original/ukbqfkkxw2o8s1gyjria-pdf.pdf)) says so, as does the Sports Dietitians Australia position statement on adolescent athletes (Desbrow et al., IJSNEM 2014, [Bond](https://research.bond.edu.au/en/publications/sports-dietitians-australia-position-statement-sports-nutrition-f/)). Rice, bread, potatoes and fruit before and between sessions. → Carb foods give the biggest snack refill (+50), and a balanced meal (bento, nikuman, oden) the most (+100: one meal fills the tank).
- **Hydration:** drink before, during and after; water first; sports drinks only for long, hot sessions ([AAP 2011, heat stress in children](https://publications.aap.org/pediatrics/article/128/3/e741/30624/Climatic-Heat-Stress-and-Exercising-Children-and-Adolescents); [Better Health Channel](https://www.betterhealth.vic.gov.au/health/healthyliving/sporting-performance-and-food)). → Drinks refill +25. A sports drink is +30 (it has a little sugar), but the copy still says water first.
- **Recovery:** protein after playing ([Sports Dietitians Australia](https://www.sportsdietitians.com.au/factsheets/fuelling-recovery/)). → Protein foods give +30. They are mainly recovery, not fuel.
- **Treats** are fine now and then but are not fuel. → +15.
- **Half-time oranges** rehydrate and give quick natural sugar (the orange's existing lesson in `goods.ts`). → Fruit +22–30. The low-fuel note teaches "Players refuel at half time".
- All copy is positive ("food is fuel"). There are no diet rules and no words about weight or calories (a test bans them).

### 1.3 Should quizzes pay coins, so children can buy fuel more easily?

**What exists today** (`lib/town/learnCoins.ts`, the HUD toast "Learning pays in full"):
- A lesson quiz pays 12 coins on the first pass, +6 for a perfect first try. That is ~239 coins an hour, the best rate in the game, and never metered.
- The daily warm-up pays 2 per right answer, at most 6 a day.
- Hidden balls pay 10, stories 10, journey stages 8, explore items 5, paths 75, and book checks 5.

So quizzes already pay.

**The research on extrinsic rewards.** Deci, Koestner & Ryan's meta-analysis of 128 experiments (Psychological Bulletin 1999, [PDF](https://home.ubalt.edu/tmitch/642/articles%20syllabus/Deci%20Koestner%20Ryan%20meta%20IM%20psy%20bull%2099.pdf)) found that expected tangible rewards undermined free-choice intrinsic motivation:
- engagement-contingent rewards: d = −0.40;
- completion-contingent: −0.36;
- performance-contingent: −0.28.

The effect was larger for children than for college students. Unexpected rewards and positive verbal feedback did not undermine motivation; feedback raised it. This is the overjustification effect, first shown with children by Lepper, Greene & Nisbett (1973).

**Weighing it:**
- *For more quiz coins:* a child short of coins could buy food sooner.
- *Against:*
  - It would turn quizzes into a fuel-earning chore, which is exactly the completion-contingent reward the meta-analysis warns about. A repeatable quiz payout would also invite answer-grinding: replaying known quizzes for coins.
  - It isn't needed. Fuel costs 3–4% of a casual player's income (§4). The free path (garden fruit) needs no coins at all.
  - Learning already pays best per minute.

**Recommendation: keep the current behaviour. Add no new or repeatable quiz coins for fuel.**
- The one-off "learning pays in full" coins stay as they are. They are a known, modest thank-you, and the game already frames them with informative feedback (what you learned).
- Fuel is deliberately **not** linked to learning in either direction. Learning never costs fuel, and learning never "buys" fuel. Football knowledge stays its own reward.

Implemented as: no change to `learnCoins.ts`; `tests/fuel.cjs` pins `activityCost(<learning>) = 0`.

## 2. Design

### 2.1 What uses fuel (per second of actual movement; standing still uses nothing)

The easier the travel, the more fuel it uses (the user's rule). Constants are in `FUEL_RATE`, `lib/town/fuel.ts`.

| Mode | Fuel / second moving | Fuel / minute | A full tank (100) lasts | At 0 fuel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jetpack (flying, or climbing with input; a hands-off hover is free) | **0.06** | 3.6 | ~28 min | Lands at the nearest safe spot (the normal landing), then walks |
| Moped | 0.045 | 2.7 | ~37 min | Steps off to walk |
| Bike | 0.035 | 2.1 | ~48 min | Steps off to walk |
| Scooter | 0.025 | 1.5 | ~67 min | Steps off to walk |
| Sprint (Shift, desktop) | 0.015 | 0.9 | ~1.9 h | Jogs at the normal walking pace |
| **Walking** | **0.003** | 0.18 | ~9 h | **Always works** (never blocked; the bar just stays at 0) |

Rates are the 30 Sep kids'-game values (first halved from jetpack 0.18 / walk 0.01, then cut again). On the jetpack, climbing or pushing with input drains too, not only flying across (the travel sampler's `airborne` flag; Town passes `rideRef.current==='jetpack'&&flight.height>.5`). **Idle never drains (3 Oct 2026, user: "standing idle… still drains the energy bar"):** the sampler also takes `driving` (joystick / move keys held, or a jetpack take-off or boost the player started; a 400 ms grace covers one HUD tick). With no input nothing is charged: a hands-off hover, a parked ride, standing with the ball, a bobbing deck, knock-backs and ramp launches are all free.
| Lessons, plays, quizzes, paths, books, stories, warm-ups | **0** | 0 | — | Never blocked |
| Jobs, fishing, talking, the garden | 0 by themselves (only the walking you do) | — | — | Never blocked |

- **Why jobs carry no flat cost:** a fuel fee on paid work would make coins cost coins, an income loop in reverse.
- **Paused (no change at all):** a lesson or field menu open, riding in a truck bed, map/fast travel, ferries and field trips (any jump bigger than the mode could travel in one tick is ignored), and a hidden tab (one tick counts at most 0.5 s).
- **Refusals:** at 0, tapping a ride or the jetpack shows "Your bike needs fuel", and the ride stays on walk. Rides come back after any snack (≥ 1 fuel).

### 2.2 What refuels

| Source | Fuel | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Konbini balanced meal (bento, loco moco, nikuman, oden) | +100 | 5–11 coins | One meal fills the tank |
| Konbini rice (onigiri, Spam musubi) | +50 | 3–8 coins | Carbohydrate = main fuel. Yaki imo is +55 (slow-release) |
| Protein (karaage, tamago sando, milk) | +30 | 3–6 | "Mostly recovery" |
| Drinks: water, teas, coconut water (Konbini or drink machine) | +25 | 3–5 | Sports drink +30 |
| Treats (melon pan, daifuku, malasada, cocoa, soda, juice) | +15 | 4–6 | A small boost: food is fun, but not fuel |
| **Fruit from the pocket** (banana 30, mango 28, orange 25, strawberry/cherry 22) | +22–30 | **free** (picked in the Community Garden, or the share from a Garden shift or Harvest day) | The zero-coin path. An eaten fruit is a 2–3 coin sale given up |
| Veg from the pocket (carrot 22, tomato/pepper/greens 20) | +20–22 | free (garden picks, Garden shift / Harvest day share) | Sweet potato must be cooked: "try the Konbini's yaki imo" |
| **Breakfast** (first moment of a new local day) | a full tank (**100**) | free | Kids'-game ease-up (user, 30 Sep). Never lowers fuel. There is no notification and nothing drains offline, so it is still no return lure |

- The Konbini's existing daily limits stay as they are: 3 food a day and 3 machine drinks a day.
- The Snacks pouch works as before. Eating from it refuels too.

### 2.3 Messages (the toast lane; one note each; no pressure)

| When | Title | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| The bar crosses 25 (once) | Fuel running low | Players refuel at half time: grab a snack at the Konbini, or eat a banana from your pocket. |
| A ride/jetpack runs out (once) | Out of fuel: time to walk | Walking always works. Eat fruit from your pocket or visit a Konbini to ride and fly again. |
| A ride tapped at 0 | Your bike needs fuel | Walk to a Konbini, a drink machine or the garden, or eat fruit from your pocket. |
| Eating | (inline) "Yum! Rice is a carbohydrate: football's main fuel… **+50 fuel.**" | The existing nutrition line, plus the fuel gained |

- Every note names a free option.
- Notes never repeat while the tank stays low, and none has a countdown or a "Buy" button.

### 2.4 The bar (coins bar) and the pocket's Fuel section

- **Coins bar:** a fuel counter sits next to coins / fish / fruit, in the same counter pill. It shows a bolt icon and the number. Low adds an amber rim and the word "Low". Empty uses a red rim, a **hollow** bolt and the word "Empty".
  - So the state never relies on colour alone, and the button's aria-label reads "fuel 0 of 100 (empty)".
  - On phones the word "coins" gives way so the pill fits between Paths and Settings at 390 px.
- **Tapping the bar** opens the island pocket, which has a new **Fuel** card:
  - the number and a meter;
  - what fuel is (the football-nutrition line);
  - "What uses fuel", with dots from jetpack ●●●●● down to walking "○ always works";
  - "Refuel", with an **Eat** button for each edible fruit/veg in the basket; with an empty basket it says "Pick fruit in the Community Garden: it's free, and you can eat it here."
  - When the tank is low or empty, the Fuel card moves to the top of the pocket.

### 2.5 The free refuel (no coins)

A child with 0 coins always has a way back, and none of it uses time against them:

| Free source | What it gives | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Breakfast** | A full tank (100) on the first moment of each new local day (`startDay`, `lib/town/fuel.ts`); a session that crosses midnight gets it too | Automatic, no notification |
| **Garden picking** | Fruit +22–30, veg +20–22 each, eaten from the pocket's Fuel card | The Community Garden (`lib/town/jobs/garden.ts`); picks go into the shared market basket |
| **The Garden shift's share** | 2 / 1 / 1 items (full / half / tip pay) of strawberry, tomato, orange, carrot or cherry, on top of the 10-coin pay | The paid Garden shift job (`lib/town/jobs/harvestShare.ts`, `docs/island-jobs.md` §13); Harvest day at Coral Cay gives a farmer's share the same way |

Walking always works at 0 fuel, so the garden is always reachable.

## 4. Economy (`node scripts/economy-sim.cjs --days 200`, 20-seed means; re-run 30 Sep 2026 with the kids'-game rates, no fountains)

**Model:**
- **Drain per session** = minutes × the travel mix (`ASSUME.travel`: 18% flying, 14% riding, 3% sprinting, 30% walking; the rest is lessons, jobs, fishing and talking, which drain nothing). At the current rates that is **casual 15, regular 20, engaged 46 fuel a session**.
- **Breakfast** fills the tank (100), and the child keeps ~10 in reserve.
- **Policies:**
  - **buy** (coin worst case): every missing bit is bought, best fuel per coin first, within the 3 food + 3 drinks daily limits. The sim reads every value from `lib/town/fuel.ts` (`foodFuel`), so it can't drift;
  - **mixed**: eat the day's garden picks first, then buy;
  - **free**: a child who never buys: garden fruit after breakfast, paying in minutes instead of coins (`ASSUME.fountainSips` is 0 since the fountains were removed).

| Player | Policy | Coins/day | Fuel coins/day | Fuel % of income | Free refuel min/day | Days short of fuel | Own everything (day) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Casual 15 min | any | 81 | 0 | 0% | 0 (breakfast covers it) | 0 | 123 |
| Regular 20 min | any | 110 | 0 | 0% | 0 | 0 | **85** ✓ |
| Engaged 45 min | any | 189 | 0 | 0% | 0 | 0 | 49 |

**Reading:**
- **The fuel coin sink is now negligible, and that is expected.** With a full tank at breakfast and the lower rates, no simulated session drains past 100. No archetype buys fuel or eats fruit for fuel, and owning everything is unchanged by fuel (regular day 85, inside 60–90).
- Fuel is now mainly the nutrition lesson ("food is fuel", "water first") plus a gentle limit on very long flights.
- Before this ease-up (rates halved only, breakfast 50) the sink was at most 3% of income (engaged, buying everything). With the first rates it was 3–9%.
- Learning stays ≥ 40% of coins, and there are no new dry spells.

**Zero-coin path stress test** (`fuelStress()`; heavy flyers, breakfast → garden fruit; re-run after the fountains were removed):

| Session | Fuel used | Garden picks | Free refuel minutes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 45 min, half flying | 83 | 0 | 0 |
| 90 min, half flying | 167 | 3 | **1.45** |
| 120 min, 60% flying | 266 | 8 | **2.2** |

Even a two-hour heavy flyer refuels for free with breakfast and about two minutes of picking in the garden.

**Guards** (`tests/economy.cjs` §9, plus the summary in `tests/fuel.cjs` §9):
- easier travel costs more (fly > moped > bike > scooter > walk);
- a 3–4 coin rice snack gives ≥ 20 fuel, and the sim's refuel values equal `lib/town/fuel.ts`;
- with every refill bought: fuel ≤ 12% of income, completion ≤ 7 days later, learning ≥ 40%, ≤ 10 empty sessions;
- the free path never leaves a day short and costs ≤ 3 min a day;
- a 90-minute heavy flyer refuels for free (breakfast + garden picks, no fountain sips) in ≤ 5 min;
- the regular player stays inside 60–90 days.

The sim's "own everything" sink totals still exclude consumables (unchanged since 29 Sep). Any fuel coins are subtracted from the balance before goal spending, so the completion days include them.
