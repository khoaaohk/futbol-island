# Coach IDP: research findings and plan (Nov 11 2026)

Status: PROPOSAL. Nothing in here is built. Owner direction so far: study game1.ai and the "compared twice / work on next" screen; the coach IDP "can be a separate part of the app" with a disclaimer.

Inputs: a read-only audit of today's IDP code (`lib/coaches/idp/*`, `components/idp/*`, `docs/idp/DESIGN.md`) and a sourced web research pass (FA / EPPP / DFB material, sports-science papers, game1.ai's own pages). Items marked (unverified) could not be confirmed from a primary source.

## 1. What exists today

- A finished single-child loop: pick an "I can..." goal (25 fixed goals, 11 skill families, FA four corners, by format), one mission a week, a face check-in, a "Talk it through" review every 42 days, celebrate, shelf. Rich parent help and a fridge card.
- No player profile (no name, age, height, position). Notes never leave the device. Privacy policy: the plan "never scores, ranks or compares children"; the game never asks a child for age.
- For a coach: give a goal by QR and print a session. No roster, no coach-side record, no ratings, no tests, no history, no custom goals.
- Rough edges: evidence is not tied to the plan's dates (lessons done earlier count); Kit Rush mapped to scanning and Breakaway to both dribbling and runs; keep-ups, Summit Kick, jobs and live matches feed nothing; unused data and dead v1 code; one stale privacy sentence (the child's check-in note is not gated).

## 2. Why IDPs fail, and the fix we design in

| Problem (evidence) | Fix in this design |
|---|---|
| Coach has no time; volunteers "simply do not have the time"; academy part-timers feel it as "another responsibility" (Coaching Manual; Leeds Beckett) | Every coach action is under a minute. Templates, one-tap check-ins, a "due this week" list. No free-text required anywhere. |
| Too many targets | One focus per player per cycle (optionally one behaviour goal). The app refuses a third. |
| Vague goals ("improve decision making") | Goals come as an observable "I can..." line + a named practice + a check date. Custom goals must fill the same three slots. |
| Written once, never revisited | The cycle is the product: 6 weeks, auto-dated, with a 60-second review that rolls into the next cycle. |
| Not linked to training | Each focus brings a session plan (the site already has 70 drills/plans) and an island mission for home. |
| Players do not own it | The child picks between two coach-suggested focuses; child's view is in their words. |
| Subjective ratings disagree between coaches (moderate agreement; ratings only match tests for the best and worst players: Dugdale 2020) | Three-point observed scale with behaviour descriptions ("not yet / sometimes / most of the time"), a change shows only when seen twice; paired with a few objective tests. |
| Early vs late developers, relative age (first-quarter births ~5x selection odds in English academies) | Birth quarter and growth context shown to the coach as cautions on any comparison. Never a label on the child. |
| Over-testing; one-off tests "can do more harm than good" (TrueSport/USADA) | Test days 2-4 a year, only tests tied to a current focus, always the same set-up. |
| Data without action | Every result ends in "Work on next": one focus, one target, one drill. |
| Rankings demotivate under-12s (mastery vs ego climate; children under ~12 do not separate effort from ability) | Child and parent views are self-referenced only: personal bests, before/after. No percentiles, ranks or forecasts outside the coach view. |

Honest limit: there is little direct research on IDP structure in football; much of this is extrapolated from goal-setting and motivation research plus practitioner experience. One small vendor-published study suggests the feedback, not the document, drives improvement (+36% with a plan, +53% with plan plus coach feedback).

## 3. What to learn from game1.ai, and what not to copy

Learn:
- One standard test day, results the same day, a plain "Work on next" with a concrete target for the next test day.
- A softer family report separate from the staff view ("not a ranking", "nobody is told they are the slowest").
- "The plan is guidance, and the coach can change or drop anything."
- Comparing twice (age and growth) so late developers are not misjudged; adult height as a range.

Do not copy:
- A potential / forecast score for children. Even the best national dataset (DFB, 14,178 under-12s) predicts adult success only modestly; a score invites early deselection. Game1's accuracy claims are vendor-only and its whitepaper is gated.
- A single "biological age 12.0, Late" figure from the Mirwald method: studies in youth soccer show such equations misclassify a third to a half of players. If used at all: a range, coach-only, with a caution.
- Percentiles on everything, squad sorting by size and speed, scored mental self-reports, camera-based capture of children (biometric questions under the amended COPPA rule, in force Apr 22 2026), training models on children's data.

## 4. Proposed product: "Coach Room" (a separate area)

Separate route and entry (e.g. `/coach`), adults only behind the existing grown-up gate, with its own terms and disclaimer. The child's game keeps today's promises unchanged.

### 4.1 Squad
- Add players by first name or nickname + shirt number. Optional, coach-entered: birth month and year, position(s), dominant foot. Nothing is asked of the child.
- Player list shows one line each: current focus, last check-in, "review due" flag.

### 4.2 One focus per cycle
- Coach picks (or the child picks from two) a focus from the existing 25 goals, or writes a custom one in three slots: "I can...", practice, check date.
- Each focus has: 3 look-fors (what the coach should see), a linked session plan, a home/island mission.
- "Super-strength" slot: one thing the player is already good at, to keep building (common academy practice; (unverified) as formal FA guidance).

### 4.3 60-second check-in (weekly, optional)
- Per player: tap one of three ("not yet / sometimes / most of the time") on the focus look-fors. Optional preset cheer. No typing.
- Squad mode: swipe through the whole squad after a session; target under 5 minutes for 14 players.

### 4.4 Test day (2-4 a year)
A small battery chosen for reliability with under-12s, cones and a phone stopwatch:
- Juggling: best of 3, any foot (weak-foot juggling tests were not reliable enough).
- Slalom dribble: timed short course, one familiarisation run first (children learn the test).
- Sprint: 10 m or 20 m.
- Change of direction: a simple 5-0-5 or modified T-test.
- Optional skill gate: wall-pass count in 30 s (practice feedback, not measurement).
Rules built in: same set-up each time (the app stores the course card and prints it), best-of-N entry, hand-timing noise (about 0.15 s) is respected: results show as "about the same / improving / new personal best" bands, with the numbers available to the coach.

### 4.5 "Work on next" card (the screen in the owner's screenshot, adapted)
- Coach view: profile across 5-6 areas drawn against the player's own previous test day (two outlines: last time and now), strengths, one focus, and targets for the next test day set just beyond test noise (e.g. juggling 3 to 5; dribble 10.4 s to about 9.8 s).
- Optional, coach-only "fair comparison" panel: birth quarter in the age group and, if the coach has entered heights over time, growth rate (cm per year) with a note when a player is in a growth spurt (expect dips in coordination). No maturity label, no single biological age. See decision D2.
- Squad comparison, if enabled (decision D1), is coach-only and shown as bands within the squad, never a rank list.

### 4.6 Player and parent view
- One focus in the child's words, the practice, personal bests with before/after, a cheer from the coach. Delivered by QR/link or printed card, as today.
- No percentiles, no comparison, no growth data, no forecast.

### 4.7 Review (every 6 weeks)
- Three taps: keep going / achieved / change focus; next cycle auto-dated. A one-page printable summary per player for the parent conversation.

### 4.8 Game evidence, fixed
- Count only evidence after the focus was set. Re-map games honestly (Pass Puzzles for scanning and space; Squad Snake for positions; Futbol Tennis for first touch; keep-ups for juggling; Summit Kick for finishing), and show it as "practised", separate from coach observation.

## 5. Data and privacy

Phase 1 keeps all squad data on the coach's device (with file export/import and print). Nothing about a named child is stored on Futbol Island servers, so no accounts are needed and the child-side policy stays true. The disclaimer covers: estimates are approximate, the plan is guidance and the coach decides, the coach is responsible for having parents' agreement to keep these notes.

A disclaimer does not by itself make server storage of children's data lawful. Phase 2 (sync across devices, parent sharing, club spaces) needs adult accounts, recorded parental consent, deletion and export, and legal review (UK Children's Code: profiling off by default, DPIA expected; amended COPPA in force Apr 22 2026). `docs/idp/DESIGN.md` section 8 already lists ten open lawyer questions.

Owner addition (Nov 11): "the coaches corner can also require an email since it's for adults". An email sign-in for the coach (magic link, no password to manage) is a reasonable gate and identity for this area: it proves an adult owns the squad, lets a coach get back in on a new phone, and is the hook for consent emails to parents later. Collecting the coach's email is ordinary adult data. It does not change the position on the players: the moment named children's records are stored on the server under that account, consent, deletion and legal review are needed. So email sign-in can arrive in phase 1 as the door, with squad data still kept on the device (optionally as an encrypted backup the server cannot read), and full sync in phase 4.

Never collected in any phase: weight, body composition, photos or video of children, a potential score.

## 6. Phases

1. **Coach Room on one device:** squad, one focus per cycle, 60-second check-in, review, printable player card. Fix evidence dating and game mapping. Remove dead v1 code. (Largest value for least risk.)
2. **Test day and Work-on-next:** battery, course cards, bands, targets, the profile chart against the player's own last result.
3. **Fair-comparison context:** birth quarter, optional height log and growth-spurt note (if D2 = yes).
4. **Sharing and sync:** accounts, consent, parent link, club space. After legal review.

## 7. Decisions for the owner

- D1. Squad comparison in the coach view: none (self-referenced only), squad bands, or percentiles?
- D2. Growth data: none, birth quarter only, or birth quarter + optional height log (coach device only)?
- D3. A forecast / potential score: recommended no.
- D4. Phase 1 storage on the coach's device only: confirm, or go straight to accounts (needs legal work first).
- D5. Custom goals allowed (with the three required slots)?
- D6. Name for the area ("Coach Room" is a placeholder) and whether it is linked from the game, the site, or both.
